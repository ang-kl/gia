## Selected Projects

**AI Readiness across Skills and Competences | Mar 2026 - Present | takearoundabout.com**

A free, browser-based tool that takes any job title and produces an assessment of how AI is reshaping each essential skill in that role. Skills are drawn directly from the ESCO v1.2 REST API - the tool never generates skill names itself. Users receive a per-skill automation rating across five levels, ready-to-use AI prompts, and mapped career progression and crossover paths. Free, no sign-up, built for a Singapore and ASEAN audience.

- Scale: directed AI coding agents through close to 40 numbered build increments across more than 80 hours of active build sessions, from an initial React prototype to a deployed product approaching 5,000 lines of React code.
- Substance: integrates the ESCO v1.2 REST API for live occupation and skill taxonomy; applies a 19-technique prompt taxonomy for per-skill AI guidance; uses a Sonnet/Haiku model split to balance quality and cost; deploys on Vercel Pro with Fluid Compute across Singapore and Japan regions; tracks eight custom analytics events; delivers six analysis views including side-by-side role comparison for up to three roles.
- Kept honest: a numbered build increment discipline applied throughout; every change verified against a live deploy before the next increment begins; the tool is explicit that results are indicative, not definitive, and that skill names come from ESCO, not from AI.

I took it on because the question it addresses - which skills AI is reshaping, and how - is one I work with professionally. Building it myself, from a practitioner’s vantage point rather than an engineer’s, was the most direct way to understand both the capability and the limits of what this kind of development can produce.

A live Telegram bot with companion Mini Apps that helps people settle the everyday question of where to eat. Directed AI coding agents through more than a hundred change iterations to ship and maintain a real deployed product. I took this on to understand the new way of building from the inside rather than the outside. I am not an engineer; the point was to learn first-hand where this technology genuinely helps and where it does not.

**Wherewithal - a personal-finance bot and Mini App | 2025 - Present | Personal project**

A Telegram bot with companion Mini App, built for my daughter, that lets her record spending in her own words and gives plain-language coaching on her money. Directed AI coding agents through dozens of iterations; live at version 0.3.0. A layered text parser - deterministic rules, then a confidence check, then an LLM fallback - reads Singlish and Gen-Z phrasing; two coaching voices handle numbers and habits separately; receipt reading, charts, spend limits and savings goals are all in-chat. The arithmetic is handled in code; the LLM phrases the advice but never owns a figure. An automated test suite checks money arithmetic and spend-limit logic against fixed expected results.
