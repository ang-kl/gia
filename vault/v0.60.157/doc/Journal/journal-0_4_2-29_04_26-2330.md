## New

### [HDR] #5 | 23:30:00 SGT 29-04-26 | v0.4.2 | n/a kb | n/a lines
- **[INTENT]** Patch fix on top of v0.4.1. Human Lead is on iPadOS with both Google Maps and Apple Maps installed. The TWA "Open in Maps" link (InfoWindow surface #3 in the diagnostic) was still opening with raw coordinates rather than the named Place page after v0.4.1.
- **[ROOT CAUSE]** v0.4.1 used the URL pattern `https://www.google.com/maps/place/?q=place_id:<id>`. iPadOS Universal Links route `https://www.google.com/maps/...` URLs to whichever Maps app the OS picks; with both Google Maps and Apple Maps installed, the link can land in Apple Maps, which does NOT understand `place_id:<id>` and degrades to coords. The pattern itself is undocumented/legacy on Google's side and not Universal-Link-stable.
- **[DELTA]**
  - **`public/app.js`** — InfoWindow link now uses Google's officially documented Maps URL Schema:
    ```
    https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=<encodedName>&query_place_id=<placeId>
    ```
    The `query=<name>` ensures the venue **name** appears in the search bar even if the OS routes to a non-Google handler. The link is rendered as a `<a href="#" data-href="…">` plus a JS click handler that calls `tg.openLink(href, { try_instant_view: false })` so Telegram's WebApp opens the URL externally rather than navigating in-place inside the Mini App webview.
  - **`package.json`** — version → 0.4.2.
- **[RISK]** Low — the new URL is the canonical Google Maps Search-action URL per `developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls`. Behaviour:
  - Safari → Google Maps web Place sheet with venue name.
  - Google Maps app installed → Universal Link routes to the Place page.
  - Apple Maps Universal Link interception → `query=<name>` keeps the **name** in the search bar; user sees the right venue rather than coords.
- **[STATUS]** PROD — auto-deploys with the next Railway build.
- **[TEST]** Human Lead to retap the InfoWindow "Open in Maps" link on iPadOS after redeploy and confirm the destination shows the venue NAME, not raw lat/lng coords.
- **[NEXT]** Phase 4 — GrabID PKCE for live quietness signal (Register Open #6).
- **[ADVICE]** Universal Links on iOS/iPadOS are a deceptively complex layer. Whenever a webview link could land in any of {Safari, app A, app B}, prefer the documented URL schema over clever shortcuts. `query_place_id` is the contract; everything else is best-effort.
