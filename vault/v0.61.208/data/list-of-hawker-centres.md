# List of Hawker Centres in Singapore

**Source:** National Environment Agency (NEA), Singapore  
**Source document:** `list-of-hcs_-25-july-2025.pdf`  
**Snapshot date:** 25 July 2025  
**Total entries:** 123 (107 markets / hawker centres + 16 new hawker centres)  
**Schema:** `s_no`, `name`, `address`, `manager_or_operator`

---

## Notes on the dataset

- The first table (**Markets / Hawker Centres**) lists 107 centres. Entries 1–27 are managed by NEA directly; entries 28–107 are managed by Town Councils as common property.
- The second table (**New Hawker Centres**) lists 16 centres operated by social enterprise or commercial operators on behalf of NEA.
- Entries marked with `#` indicate that tenancies are managed by FairPrice Group Hawker Centre Pte. Ltd. on behalf of NEA.
- Bukit Timah Market (S/No 6) is closed for redevelopment till 2029 (tentative).
