# Journal — soleat v0.61.120 (chat free-text whitelist for "<cuisine> + <food noun>")

> Operator-reported defect: typing **"western cuisine nearby"** in the
> Telegram chat free-text path returned the food-nudge decline
> *"🍛 Please try a dish name, cooking method, or food term — e.g. Mee

## New

### [HDR] #321 | 12:39 SGT 24-05-26 | v0.61.120 — chat free-text whitelist for `<cuisine> + <food noun>` queries | ~6 kb | 4 files | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Restore service for legitimate cuisine-browse queries
  that pair a cuisine word (catalogue cuisine or umbrella like
  "western"/"asian") with a food-specific noun
  (cuisine/food/restaurant/eatery/dining/nearby). Currently every such
  query gets declined by the v0.60.216 gate because Gemini Flash sees
  no fitting bucket. Preserve the v0.60.216 protection for genuinely
  off-topic input ("tell me a joke", "weather in tokyo").

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`freetext-classify.js`** — new `looksLikeCuisineBrowse(text)` helper.
    Returns true iff the text contains BOTH:
    - a cuisine word — catalogue cuisine (via
      `cuisine-family.cuisineFamily()`, substring match) OR an umbrella
      from `CUISINE_UMBRELLA_WORDS` (`western`, `asian`, `fusion`,
      `continental`, `international`, `pan asian`, `eclectic`, `global`)
    - a food-specific noun from `FOOD_SPECIFIC_NOUNS`
      (`cuisine`/`food`/`restaurant`/`eatery`/`dining`/`dish`/`menu`/
      `kopitiam`/`hawker`/`foodcourt`/`nearby`/`near`/`around` + plurals)
    Exported alongside the existing `looksLikeQuestion`/`looksLikeTransport`.
    Conservative by design: bare cuisine words ("italian") and bare
    food nouns ("restaurant") do NOT trip the whitelist — only the
    pairing does.
  - **`index.js`** (~7727) — chat free-text food-relatedness gate
    (v0.60.216 block) now calls `looksLikeCuisineBrowse(text)` first.
    On a hit the Gemini classifier is skipped entirely and the path
    proceeds straight to `runFreeTextSearch`. Logs
    `[free-text] D773 cuisine-browse whitelist hit — skipping food-relatedness gate for "<text>"`
    on bypass.
  - **`__tests__/freetext-classify.test.js`** — new
    `describe('looksLikeCuisineBrowse', …)` block with 7 specs:
    the reported regression `"western cuisine nearby"` → true; umbrella
    + food noun (asian restaurant, fusion dining, pan asian cuisine);
    catalogue cuisine + food noun (italian restaurant, japanese food
    near me, european dining around me); bare cuisine words (italian,
    japanese, western) → false; bare food nouns → false; v0.60.216
    off-topic negatives (tell me a joke, weather in tokyo, how is the
    stock market) → false; blanks/nonsense.
  - **`package.json`** — `0.61.119 → 0.61.120` (PATCH).
