## New

### [HDR] #314 | 07:55 SGT 23-05-26 | v0.61.114 — Cuisine map zoom-11 lock + centroid | ~3 kb | 2 files | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Two operator asks for the Cuisine TMA Google-Maps panel:
  1. Lock the zoom to exactly **11** on initial load **and** on every
     new search result, instead of the v0.61.110 ceiling-of-11 +
     fitBounds behaviour (which still zoomed out below 11 when the
     result spread was wide, and which still added a 60 px frame
     around the cluster).
  2. Drop the "centralised" fitBounds framing entirely — show the
     results in view without the embedded-map frame.

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`web/cuisine/src/v2/components/MapPanel.jsx`**
    - `initMap()` — initial `zoom: 14` → `zoom: 11`. So the first
      paint already lands in the same Singapore-wide framing the
      result map will use, avoiding a zoom-out flicker the moment
      the first search lands.
    - `syncMarkers()` fit block (the `bounds.isEmpty()` branch
      from v0.61.107 / v0.61.110) rewritten. Out: `setOptions({
      maxZoom: 11 })` + `fitBounds(bounds, 60)` + the post-`idle`
      `setOptions({ maxZoom: null })` release. In: a centroid of
      the finite-`lat`/-`lng` venues (`mean(v.lat), mean(v.lng)`)
      followed by `setCenter(centroid)` and `setZoom(11)`. The
      anchor pin is intentionally **not** in the centroid — by
      operator choice the camera tracks the result cluster, not
      the search anchor. When `venues` is empty (initial paint
      before any search, or a search that returned nothing) the
      centre falls back to `searchCenter || userLoc`, still at
      `z11`. The same-`venues` re-render branch (e.g. `userLoc`
      resolved) keeps the user's current zoom and just `panTo`s
      the centroid, matching v0.61.107 behaviour.
    - The `bounds = new LatLngBounds()` accumulator + the
      `bounds.extend()` calls in the per-marker loop and the
      anchor-pin block are now unreferenced by the fit block.
      Left in place to minimise diff blast radius — they are
      cheap and any future re-introduction of a bounds-driven
      view (e.g. a manual "frame all results" button) can use
      them directly.
  - **`package.json`** — `0.61.113` → `0.61.114` (PATCH).

- **[TRADE-OFF (operator-acknowledged)]** At `z11` the Singapore
  viewport is roughly 30 km wide. The centroid keeps results
  inside the viewport whenever the spread is ≲ 15 km from the
  centroid — which is almost all of Singapore. Two known failure
  modes (acknowledged in the AskUserQuestion preview text):
  - **Bimodal results** (e.g. half in Tuas, half in Changi):
    centroid lands in empty water in the middle; the user pans
    to one side or the other.
  - **Anchor far from cluster** (e.g. anchor in Marina Bay, all
    results in JB): the "you are here" anchor pin may sit
    off-screen. The operator picked centroid over `searchCenter`
    knowing this — the prior fitBounds behaviour solved this case
    at the cost of the 60 px frame the operator wants gone.
