- **[INTENT]** A long, mostly-FE polishing session on the Cuisine/Hawker/Transport Telegram Mini Apps,
  driven PR-by-PR from operator screenshots: redesign the eatery result card (distance into the meta row;
  drop the confusing walk-minutes; re-order + collapse rows), then turn the selected card into a
  **music-app carousel** over the map, then move toward a **floating layout** (floating header + footer
  carousel + a search FAB). Interleaved with correctness fixes: a venue-pin tap that escaped the TMA on
  desktop, an SG cuisine search drifting to foreign cities, wrong Bukit Panjang LRT coordinates, and a
  GPS reverse-geocode that resolved to the planning area instead of the building/street. Plus skeuomorphic
  map pop-up cards, line-coloured station-code chips, equal-width region pills, a default-zoom bump, and a
  custom centre-map (↹) control replacing Google's native pad. Seven doc-only PRs (vibe-journal refresh,
  Soleat primer, UI audit, four MVP mockups) framed the rework.
