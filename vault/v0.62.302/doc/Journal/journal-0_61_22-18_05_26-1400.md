- **[INTENT]** Operator, on the Google-map popups across all three TMAs: *"Can the google
  pin's information be curve square light/dark background sensitive, not to washout many
  texts… Can it have a close button… After tap elsewhere in the google map the popup be
  closed."*

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`web/{cuisine,hawker,transport}/src/.../lib/mapOverlays.js`** (byte-identical ×3) —
    new exported `infoPalette()` returns a light or dark colour set keyed off
    `window.Telegram.WebApp.colorScheme`. `infoCard` is now exported, theme-aware, and
    renders an in-card **✕** (top-right) that calls `window.__giaMapInfoClose`. Every
    popup builder (`busInfoHtml`, the `attachAmenityPins` exit/taxi/carpark/station cards,
    `attractionInfo`) recolours its secondary text + links from the palette so nothing
    washes out. The overlay-controller `InfoWindow` is constructed with
    `headerDisabled: true`, a `map` click closes it, and a `closeInfo()` method is exposed.
  - **`web/transport/src/components/MrtMapPanel.jsx`**,
    **`web/cuisine/src/v2/components/MapPanel.jsx`**,
    **`web/hawker/src/components/HawkerMapPanel.jsx`** — each imports `infoCard` /
    `infoPalette` and re-wraps its own popups (station / venue + anchor + incident /
    hawker-centre) in the themed card; their `InfoWindow`s get `headerDisabled: true`; each
    `initMap` registers `window.__giaMapInfoClose` and a `map` click listener that closes
    both the panel's InfoWindow and the overlay controller's.
