- **[MECHANISM]** `instruction/Taiwan.js` is a bare array literal of 196 venue objects in venue-award-schema.v0_1 shape (no locale fields). Copied **byte-for-byte** into `TW-michelin.js`. Five of the seven cities (Taipei→TPE, Kaohsiung→KHH, Taichung→TXG, Tainan→TNN, Hsinchu→HSZ) were already mapped; **three were not** — `New Taipei`, `Hsinchu City`, `Hsinchu County` — so the loader would have thrown. Added to `michelin-data.js` `CITY_IATA`: `'new taipei':'TPE'` (Greater Taipei), `'hsinchu city':'HSZ'`, `'hsinchu county':'HSZ'`. `cities.js` (the OTHER-mode picker) and `iata-cities.js` are **not** touched — `CITY_IATA` is the loader's gate (and already a superset of the picker: keelung/jiufen/sun-moon-lake etc. live there too); the picker already carries the Taiwan majors and all three codes (TPE/HSZ) exist in `iata-cities.js`.
