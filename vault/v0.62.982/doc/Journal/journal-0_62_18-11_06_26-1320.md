- **[INTENT / DIAGNOSIS]** "Legoland still spills far." Investigation showed the **name-confusion the
  operator feared is already largely prevented**: the JB focus chips use **fixed coordinates**
  (`jb-focus-points.js` — Legoland = 1.4296/103.6321) and the typed-name box's autocomplete is
  **Malaysia-restricted** (`includedRegionCodes:['MY']` + a JB locationBias) → it resolves *Legoland
  Malaysia*, never Legoland California, and the food query never contains the place name. The real spill
  has two causes: **(A)** v0.62.17's search-center fix **isn't deployed yet** (current build 0.62.16
  still snaps Legoland's coords to JB CBD); **(B)** even with correct coords, the **30 km JB radius**
  draws the dense JB-CBD / Mount-Austin cluster into a Legoland search (nearest-first floods it).

- **[DELTA — B: tight radius per focus pick]** `location-mode.jbFocusNear({lat,lng})` detects (by
  **coordinates**, within 400 m — never the name) when the search is anchored ON one of the 5 registered
  JB sub-locations (Legoland / Bukit Indah / CBD / Southkey / Mt Austin). In the cuisine-search route
  (after the radius/cap block), a JB focus pick is bounded to a **tight ring**: `searchRadius → ≤6 km`,
  `anchorCap = 12 km` (the widen ladder / outer-ring re-fetch may reach 12 km only on a thin pool) →
  results are **near the pick**, not the 30 km metro (`[Cuisine-TMA] D784`). Non-focus JB searches
  (plain pill / device GPS elsewhere in JB) keep the 30 km behaviour.

- **[DELTA — C: Johor-extent guard]** `location-mode.insideJohorExtent({lat,lng})` (Johor bbox lat
  1.20–2.55, lng 102.50–104.50). A region='JB' search whose anchor falls **outside** the Johor extent is
  logged (`D785 JB-guard`) as a possible name/phrase mis-resolution — defence-in-depth on top of the
  MY-restricted autocomplete and the 150 km `JB_FALLBACK` downgrade.

- **[DELTA — D: codify the rule]** `doc/SearchStrategy/data/search-modes.json` tree gains step 10: *a
  search is anchored by **coordinates + a geofence, never the bare place name/phrase**; a registered JB
  sub-location pick tightens the radius; an out-of-Johor JB anchor is flagged.* Regenerated
  `search-strategy.md` + the hosted mind-map `public/doc/search-strategy.html`.

- **[DELTA — A: deploy]** v0.62.17 (the SG-bbox carve-out that stops the JB_CBD snap) is **merged**;
  Railway redeploy + Telegram cache refresh are pending — that lands the coord fix this PR's radius
