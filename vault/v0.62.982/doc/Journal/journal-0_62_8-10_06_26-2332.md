- **[WHY]** Verified (Round 5, free DuckDB count over Foursquare): Bangkok has **173k restaurants /
  ~10k Japanese**; the app shows **5** purely from the 60-cap + rating-floor + geo-ring + first-tap-5
  (Round 4 proved it's NOT a timeout). Foursquare OS Places (Apache-2.0, 100% cuisine-labelled) is the
  free base; ratings come later, lazily, from Google. Full advisory in the plan file.

- **[DELTA — new files]**
  - **`scripts/build-cuisine-corpus.py`** — offline harvester (Python+DuckDB; **build-time only, adds
    NO dependency to the Node/Railway app**). Reads the free FSQ parquet (source.coop), filters
    Bangkok-bbox restaurants, drops `date_closed`, dedups by `fsq_place_id`, stores leaf category labels.
    `$0` (no Google). **Ran it → `data/corpus/bangkok.json.gz` (7.2 MB gz, 165,032 venues)** + `manifest.json`
    + `NOTICE.txt` (Apache-2.0 attribution). Runtime: ~22 min (full-row pull of 81 shards over https).
  - **`corpus-cuisine-map.js`** — single source of truth mapping FSQ leaf labels → our **69 cuisine
    slugs** (longest-token-first, consumes matches so "South Indian" → `south-indian` only). Generic
    (Noodle/Seafood/Restaurant) → `[]` (browse-only). Verified on the shard: **54% of rows carry ≥1
    slug; 8,877 Japanese.**
  - **`corpus-store.js`** — Redis-Geo serving layer (mirrors `vault.js` GEOSEARCH + `sync-vault.js`
    GEOADD/HSET): `loadCityCorpus` (batched, idempotent, skip-if-loaded), `queryCorpus` (radius +
    cuisine-slug filter, nearest-first, returns the pipeline venue shape with `placeId:null`/`rating:null`
    until enriched), `warmCitiesInBackground` (never blocks boot), `corpusCityForPoint`. **Not imported
    by `index.js` yet — dead-safe until Phase 2.**
  - **`__tests__/corpus-cuisine-map.test.js`** (10) + **`__tests__/corpus-store.test.js`** (5, stub Redis).

- **[VERIFICATION (sandbox)]** `node --check` clean (both modules); `python3 ast.parse` clean. Shard
