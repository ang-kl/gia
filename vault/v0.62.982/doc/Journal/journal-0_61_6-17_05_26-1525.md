## New

### [HDR] #209 | 15:25:00 SGT 17-05-26 | v0.61.6 — hawker-centre nearby transit (station + bus stops) | 3 files | PR 3 of 4 | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Each hawker-centre card should surface the nearest MRT station and the 2 nearest
  bus stops so a user can see how to get there at a glance.

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`index.js`** — new `GET /api/hawker/centre-transit?lat=&lng=`: returns
    `{ station, busStops }`. `station` is the nearest **operational** MRT station, computed
    offline from `data/mrt-coords.json` (`nearestStationTo()` — no Google Places call), with
    its codes, name and line codes. `busStops` is the 2 nearest bus stops via
    `transport.nearestStops()` (Redis GEOSEARCH over the boot-cached LTA bus-stop set).
    Redis-cached per rounded coord, 30 min.
  - **`web/hawker/src/App.jsx`** — a new `transitByName` state; an effect fetches
    `/api/hawker/centre-transit` for every centre of the active region (parallel, lazy, merged
    into state). Each centre card now renders, below the stall/status line, a `🚉` nearest-
    station row and up to two `🚌` bus-stop rows — every one a hyperlink to that
    station/stop's location in Google Maps.
  - **`package.json`** — `0.61.5 → 0.61.6` (PATCH — hawker nearby-transit).
  - Operating status and Address were already on the card (`formatStalls` + the address line)
    — no change needed for those.
