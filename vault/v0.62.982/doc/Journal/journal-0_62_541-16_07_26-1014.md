- **[INTENT].** Outside SG, ring 3 must appear **only** when result(s) fall **outside** the 2 km ring
  (ring 2), placed at the farthest such result — so the results themselves bound it, and it's never
  drawn inside ring 2.

  drew a ring 3 even when the farthest result was **inside** ring 2 (e.g. a 1200 m farthest result →
  a ring 3 *smaller* than the 2 km ring). That contradicted the operator's model.
