> - **`ciorbă`** — the Romanian soup family, by far the most-named-on-menus category.
> - **`papanași`** — iconic cheese-donut dessert with a distinctive name that won't collide with other cuisines.
> - **`restaurant românesc`** — shorter / more name-form generic than the original `bucătărie românească`.
>
> Net: 4 → 6 seeds for Romanian. Expected uplift on the next variance run: 68 → ~120+ total across 8 cities.
