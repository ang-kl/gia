### [HDR] #39 | 17:30:00 SGT 30-04-26 | v0.24.0 | n/a kb | n/a lines
- **[INTENT]** Per Human Lead spec, ship `/surprise`:
  > "based on last 4 days good review, 1.5 km in radius, starting from 1.5 km away from current location, it must be 4.3 and above google rating, hidden less than 50 people review, range between 10-30 dollars, open now or two hours later except closing time, highlight one to 3 special dishes and why people order. whether booking is required."
  Defaults accepted in plan-mode: **annulus geometry** (1.5–3 km from origin, not a thin ring). Returns ONE venue (a "surprise" not a list).
- **[DELTA]**
  - **NEW** `surprise.js` — `findSurprise({lat, lng})` end-to-end pipeline:
    1. Places Nearby Search (`places:searchNearby`, includedTypes: restaurant/cafe/bar/meal_takeaway, radius 3 km, max 20 results, cheap field-mask).
    2. Cheap pre-filter on the response: `OPERATIONAL` business status, `rating ≥ 4.3`, `userRatingCount` in `(0, 50)`, `priceLevel ≤ MODERATE` (proxy for $10–$30 entrée).
    3. Distance gate via haversine: keep only venues with `1500 ≤ distanceM ≤ 3000`.
    4. For up to 6 highest-rated survivors, fetch Place Details (full field-mask incl. `reviews`, `currentOpeningHours`, `regularOpeningHours`).
    5. **Opening gate** — `passesOpeningGate`: open now AND not within 30 min of close, OR opens within next 2 hours.
    6. **Recent-review gate** — `passesRecentReviewGate`: at least one review in the last 4 days with `rating ≥ 4`.
    7. First survivor wins. Hand its summary to Gemini for `{dishes: [1-3], why_ordered: string, booking_required: bool}` enrichment.
  - **NEW** `priceLevelToInt`, `haversine`, `nextOpenTime`, `nextCloseTime` helpers in `surprise.js` (kept local — different shape from `vibe-suggest.js#haversineMeters` which only takes 2 simple `{lat,lng}` records).
  - **`index.js`** —
    - + `bot.onText(/^\/surprise…)` slash handler.
    - + `routeMenuCommand`'s `'surprise'` case (so a future menu tile can dispatch).
    - + `runSurpriseCommand(chatId)` — uses cached location, prompts to share if missing, marks processing, calls `findSurprise`, calls `deliverSurprise`.
    - + `deliverSurprise(chatId, venue)` — Markdown-formatted surprise card:
      ```
      🎲 *<name>*
      <area>
      ⭐<rating> (<count> reviews) · Open now · <km> km away

      🍴 *Try the:*
      • <dish 1>
      • <dish 2>

      _<why people order>_

      📅 Booking is usually advised at peak.
      ```
      Inline buttons: 🚗 Directions, 🔍 Google. Falls back to plain text if Markdown errors on a stray char.
    - + `setMyCommands` row: `surprise — One hidden gem 1.5–3 km away`.

- **[KNOWN GAPS]**
  - Price band "$10–$30" mapped to `priceLevel ≤ MODERATE`. Places API doesn't expose absolute SGD; this is the closest available signal. False positives expected at edges.
  - "Last 4 days good review" relies on Places returning at least one recent review — venues with very low review counts (~5) may legitimately miss this even when known to be good. Conservative on purpose: surprise picks should have *fresh* social signal.
  - Cost per `/surprise` ~$0.10–$0.12 (1 nearby search + up to 6 details + 1 Gemini). Significantly above `/eat` baseline. Documented; cache deferred.
  - `nextOpenTime` reads `regularOpeningHours.periods` as local SGT — fine for SG; would mis-time across timezones.
