> Operator review of the v0.61.x Mini-App maps raised three items: dark-mode control-button

## New

### [HDR] #218 | 06:23:00 SGT 18-05-26 | v0.61.16 — map control buttons + Transport station-detail view | ~14 kb | 6 files | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Operator review of the v0.61.x maps: (1) the Google-map control buttons (zoom
  `＋` / `－`, expand `⤢`) are near-invisible in Telegram dark mode — they used theme-adaptive
  `tg-card` colours over the always-light Google map; (2) replace the focus-reset `Overview ↺`
  button with a toggle back to the last zoomed-in view (which includes the focused line, if
  one is selected), greyed when there is nothing to toggle — Transport TMA only; (3) selecting
  a station should show its exits (pins labelled with the exit letter/number), bus stops
  (`№<code>`), taxi stand / pick-up point, and carparks (`🅿️`), and hide every other station
  except the one immediately before and after on the line. Triggered by **both** a pin tap
  and the focused-line panel's station picker.

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`web/transport/src/components/MrtMapPanel.jsx`** — control buttons pinned to fixed
    `bg-white text-gray-900` styling (dark-mode contrast fix). The `Overview ↺` button is
    replaced by an **Overview toggle**: entering Overview stashes the exact viewport
    (`savedViewRef`) and frames the whole network; "Back" restores it (`skipFitRef` suppresses
    the one re-fit); the button greys out when no line/station is focused. New
    **station-detail view** — `computeDetail()` resolves the selected station's prev/next on
    the line via `lineStationsFull()`; `renderPins` shows only those three stations;
    `renderAmenityPins()` draws exit / bus-stop / taxi / carpark markers (`amenityPinNode`)
    from `/api/transport/station-context`. Tapping any pin selects it (`onStationSelect`).
    The v0.61.14 6 km radius mode (`STATION_FOCUS_M`, `splitByRadius`, `metresBetween`,
    `faded` dimming) is **removed** — superseded by this narrower view (see Deleted).
  - **`web/transport/src/App.jsx`** — `onResetFocus` prop dropped; `onStationSelect`
    (= `handleSelectStation`) wired so a map-pin tap also drives `focusedStation`.
  - **`web/transport/src/i18n.js`** — `mrt.overview` ("Overview"), `mrt.backToView`
    ("Back ↩"), `mrt.carparks` (en + fr).
  - **`web/hawker/src/components/HawkerMapPanel.jsx`** /
    **`web/cuisine/src/v2/components/MapPanel.jsx`** — same dark-mode control-button
    contrast fix (3 buttons each).
  - **`index.js`** — `/api/transport/station-context` now also returns `carparks` (within
    400 m, via `carpark.allPoints()`; locations only; `[]` without `LTA_ACCOUNT_KEY`).

## Deleted

The v0.61.14 6 km station-focus map mode is superseded by the v0.61.16 station-detail view
(operator-requested narrower prev/this/next framing). Removed from `MrtMapPanel.jsx`:
`STATION_FOCUS_M` (6000 m constant), `splitByRadius()`, `metresBetween()`, `activeStationFocus()`,
and the `faded` opacity argument of `stationDotNode`. Behaviour described verbatim in
`journal-0_61_14-17_05_26-2016.md` HDR #217 [DELTA] — "all lines shown, pins/line within 6 km
opaque, beyond 60 % transparent, the selected station a larger centre pin". Preserved here per
Recipe AU-5; the `focusedStation` prop and the station-picker trigger are retained.
