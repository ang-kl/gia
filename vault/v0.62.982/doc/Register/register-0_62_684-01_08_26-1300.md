## Note

D-51 **knowingly reverses v0.62.577**, which removed a `max-w-[22rem]` per-card cap because it gave
"more, flatter cards instead of a prominent centre + two glass half-peeks." At 320px an iPad mini in
landscape shows ~3.0 cards where the 44% rung showed ~2.3. The conflict was surfaced to the operator
twice — once as a caution, once inside the written spec — before approval, so this is recorded as a
deliberate supersession rather than an oversight. If the peek aesthetic is missed on device, the
lever is the 20rem cap, not the ladder (which is now gone).

Worth noting what the audit found that the operator had NOT reported: phone landscape rendered a
**609px** card, wider than the iPad mini portrait case that prompted the work. It was invisible
because nobody had tabulated the widths — the same "measure, don't infer" pattern that produced
C-95's 5px gap and disproved the C-94 hypothesis.
