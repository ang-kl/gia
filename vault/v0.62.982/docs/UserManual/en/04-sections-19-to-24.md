# Soleat User Manual — Sections 19 to 24

## 19. Singapore Hawker Centres & Food Centres

Singapore Hawker Centres & Food Centres help you explore one of the most important everyday food settings in Singapore.

Use this feature when you want hawker-centre context rather than only individual eatery results. It is useful for local meals, casual dining, food trails, nearby options, and planning around stations, neighbourhoods, or attractions.

### 19.1 When to use hawker-centre search

Use hawker-centre search when you want to:

- find hawker centres near an area;
- compare food-centre options;
- plan a local food stop;
- look for practical meal choices;
- connect food planning with nearby transport; or
- save a hawker centre into Sketchbook for later.

### 19.2 Use `/hawker`

To start from Telegram chat, type:

```text
/hawker
```

Where supported, Soleat may open or return hawker-centre information for Singapore.

### 19.3 Read hawker-centre results

A hawker-centre result may help you understand the centre name, area, map position, and nearby context.

When planning, check whether the hawker centre fits your route, timing, weather, transport, and meal purpose.

### 19.4 Use hawker centres with Cuisine Search

Hawker centres can be useful when you are searching for local food, Singapore classics, practical lunch, casual dinner, or food-trail stops.

If an individual eatery result is too narrow, a nearby hawker centre may give more choice.

## 20. Singapore Train Network / Transport

Singapore Train Network / Transport helps you judge whether a food place is practical to reach by public transport.

It is useful when the food decision depends on station access, route convenience, nearby bus stops, live arrivals, walking distance, or service status.

### 20.1 When to use transport information

Use transport information when you want to:

- check MRT or LRT context;
- see whether a place is near a train station;
- review station-related travel information;
- check nearby bus stops and live bus arrivals where available;
- decide whether a place fits your route; or
- plan food stops before or after travel.

### 20.2 Use `/transport`

To start from Telegram chat, type:

```text
/transport
```

Where supported, Soleat may show transport options such as train, bus, walking, or driving context.

### 20.3 Use train context with food search

Train context is useful when you are deciding between similar food options.

A place with slightly lower food interest may still be better if it is easier to reach, closer to a station, or more convenient for your next appointment.

### 20.4 Transport tips

Use transport information together with map view.

For example:

```text
Search food
        ↓
Check map view
        ↓
Check train station context
        ↓
Check station exits or nearby bus stops
        ↓
Choose, refine, or save
```

## 21. Singapore Train Station Exits

Singapore Train Station Exits help you plan the walking part of a trip.

A place may be near a station but still inconvenient if the wrong exit adds walking time, road crossings, shelter issues, or confusion.

### 21.1 When station exits matter

Station exits are useful when you are:

- meeting someone near a station;
- walking from MRT to an eatery;
- planning in rain or hot weather;
- choosing between nearby food places;
- trying to reduce walking time;
- planning with children, elderly family members, or luggage; or
- arranging a food stop before the next activity.

### 21.2 Use station exits with map view

Station exits are most useful when seen together with map context.

Use Google Map View to compare the eatery, station, exit direction, nearby roads, bus stops, car parks, or landmarks.

### 21.3 Use station exits with Sketchbook

If you are planning a food trail or trip, save eatery cards into Sketchbook and add notes about useful station exits.

This helps you avoid rechecking the same route later.

### 21.4 Station timing information

Where available, Soleat may provide station-related timing information such as first-train, last-train, or service-timing context.

Use this as travel planning support and verify critical timing before making time-sensitive decisions.

## 22. Singapore Bus Information

Singapore Bus Information helps you understand nearby bus options around a place, station, or food area.

This is useful when the nearest train station is not enough, or when the last part of the trip is better by bus.

### 22.1 When to use bus information

Use bus information when you want to:

- see nearby bus stops;
- check bus numbers serving an area;
- see real-time bus arrivals where available;
- compare bus access near train stations;
- decide whether a place is convenient without a long walk; or
- plan a food stop around public transport.

### 22.2 Bus information near train stations

Bus information is especially helpful near Singapore train stations.

A station may get you close to an area, but a nearby bus may make the final route easier, especially in heat, rain, or unfamiliar neighbourhoods.

### 22.3 Use bus information with food search

When comparing eateries, bus context can help decide whether a place is practical.

For example, if two options look similar, the one with better bus access or a shorter walk may be the better choice for the moment.

### 22.4 Bus information tips

Check bus arrival information when timing matters.

For critical trips, always allow extra time and verify the route before going.

## 23. Singapore Car Parks

Singapore Car Parks help you decide whether a food place is practical if you are driving.

This is useful for family meals, rainy days, late dinners, errands, or trips where public transport is less convenient.

### 23.1 Use `/carpark`

To start from Telegram chat, type:

```text
/carpark
```

Where available, Soleat may show nearby car parks and live available-lot context.

### 23.2 When to check car parks

Check car parks when you:

- are driving to eat;
- are planning with family;
- need to minimise walking;
- are going during peak meal periods;
- are visiting a crowded area;
- are planning dinner, supper, or weekend outings; or
- want to compare convenience before deciding.

### 23.3 Use car park context with eatery cards

A food place may look attractive, but poor parking can make it unsuitable for the moment.

Use car park context together with opening hours, weather, map layers, and transport options.

### 23.4 Car park tips

Live availability can change quickly.

Use car park information as a decision aid, not a guarantee. If timing is critical, verify before going.

## 24. Weather

Weather helps you decide whether a food place is practical now.

In Singapore, heat, rain, and short-term weather changes can affect whether a place is easy to reach, whether walking is comfortable, and whether outdoor or unsheltered routes are suitable.

### 24.1 Use `/weather`

To start from Telegram chat, type:

```text
/weather
```

Where available, Soleat may show current weather or short-term forecast information.

### 24.2 When weather matters

Check weather when you are:

- walking from a station;
- choosing between indoor and outdoor routes;
- planning a hawker-centre visit;
- deciding whether to drive;
- planning with children, elderly family members, or visitors;
- going between multiple food stops; or
- planning around exposed areas.

### 24.3 Use weather with transport and map view

Weather is most useful when combined with practical context.

For example:

```text
Check food options
        ↓
Check map view
        ↓
Check station exit or bus access
        ↓
Check weather
        ↓
Choose the practical option
```

### 24.4 Weather tips

If weather makes a good food option inconvenient, save it into Sketchbook for later.

Choose the place that fits the current situation, not only the place that looks best on food interest alone.
