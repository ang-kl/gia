# Journal — soleat v0.62.25 (Michelin: reachable George Town jump + deadline no longer burns the walk)

> Two operator-reported Michelin defects, one PR:
> **(A)** *"Michelin listing split into two locations for Malaysia doesn't show George Town after
> finishing Kuala Lumpur — I thought we settled this."* v0.61.445 scoped the walk to ONE city to
> stop the map framing two cities, and was supposed to surface the other city as a pickable hint —
> but the client received `nationRemaining` as **dead state** (never rendered) and the partial hint
> was gated on `remaining > 0`, so at KL-exhaustion nothing pointed to George Town. Now the server
> sends a per-city breakdown (`otherCities` + ISO `countryCode`) and the TMA renders **tappable**
> "📍 N more in George Town →" nudges (shown even when the in-city walk is exhausted) that
> re-anchor there and search.
> **(B)** *"After 3 taps of Michelin Singapore it shows zero — there are 130, I thought Michelin is
> special."* The walk DOES paginate the full 130 (verified: 130 distinct entryKeys, 12/tap,
> skipCap, 1h TTL) — but when the route's 20s deadline fired mid-resolution it answered with a
> degraded empty payload while the handler **still advanced the walk seen-set**, silently burning
> the 12 unseen entries the user never received. A few slow taps therefore read as "zero" while the
> walk paged deep into the list. Guarded: if the response is already out, don't record + don't
> double-send, so the next tap re-serves the batch.

> Commit 692 / journal 675 / build 425 (web/ changed). PATCH 0.62.24 → 0.62.25 (renumbered: #986
> took 0.62.24 while this was in flight).
