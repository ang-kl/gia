  - **`index.js` — recents writer**: now **always** reverse-geocodes (24 h-cached on a ~10 m grid, fire-and-forget) and **prefers** `tidyRecentLabel` over the client's short label / city pick; stores **`geo.countryCode`** so the **flag renders for every country** (JP/KR/HK/…).
