## New / expanded features (by arc)

### F-A · Michelin, multi-country
The Michelin surface grew from SG-only to **11 markets** — TH, MY, JP, KR, TW, VN, HK, MO, PH, CN (+ SG) — ~**2,000 curated venues** with **native-script names/addresses** (kanji, hangul, hanzi, etc.). Results are **country-aware** (edition line per country), shown in a **city-grouped display** (per-`awardCity` sections, city-jump rows that read `count/total`, set-city + fit-bounds), with a city-aware "Explore N more in <City> · M across <Country>" hint. The redundant "Showing first 12" and the 🇸🇬 flag chip were dropped. *(#830–#865, #960–#974)*

### F-B · Arrival Plate — "What to order"
A per-city **curated dish plate**: native dish names with tier + claim, **📜 history fact-cards** (en/fr), and **evidence-split** dish results (which venues actually serve the dish). **⭐ Recommend** overlays the city's classics; plate rows show **🔍 find eateries**; the ⭐ summary **names the dish** ("6 of 12 places here serve Bún chả"). City-plate **grouping** added. Full curation pass: **126 cities / 279 dish rows**, local-language-first. *(#991–#1003, #1006–#1009)*

### F-C · Location coherence
**Per-device** location + country keys (one chatId, many devices); **explicit picks latch** and win over device-sync; the **location-mismatch popup was removed** in favour of **country-strict auto-use**; selecting a **country anchors to its capital**; recents drawer (LRU 20) stores full street address + correct country flag; map **previews** on city pick and **commits on 🔍**; "Search area set to …" chat confirmation on a deliberate pick. *(#863–#928, #988–#998)*

### F-D · Rating as a filter
Guarded **≥3.7** minimum-rating floor across all eatery surfaces; **per-chat rating preference** + **`/rating`** command (in the public slash menu, EN+FR); rating is a **real search criterion** with a compact 2-up panel (red→green Save); Michelin disables rating; "No rating" = new-or-null. *(#925, #941–#949)*

### F-E · Prices in your currency (FX)
Venue prices convert to the **device home currency** (USD-pivot table; Alpha Vantage + Frankfurter fallback; 15-day cache; +2.8 % markup), following the iOS **Region** (not Language). VND rendering (~8,000× error) fixed. *(#875, #876, #891, #903, #971)*

### F-F · Fun-facts & internationalisation
**Global + device-localised** fact cards rotating every 15 s; **native-script venue names** with a readable Gemini-romanised line keyed to the device language; global **weather** via Open-Meteo. *(#874, #892–#900, #895)*

### F-G · Durian / fruits belt
Durian belt extended to **7 countries** (incl. VN "sầu riêng"); **generic-term fallback** so Brunei et al. stop returning 0; **soft 3.7** durian rating + cultivar name-gate; Fruits pill unlocked everywhere while durian/durian-pastry stay belt-gated. *(#911–#932, #977–#982)*

### F-H · Progressive results
Cuisine results **stream** (NDJSON base→patch→done); the first batch **auto-grows 6→12** in two waves; fast/slow enrichment split. *(#983–#985)*

### F-I · "Newness", unified
One **newness** rule across the **New pill**, **`/hidden`**, and curated cards (review-time refute, **121-day** days-precise); `/hidden` owner-gated and rendering the same rich card as `/search`. *(#818, #826, #864, #940, #965)*

### F-J · Surfaces & misc
Public **`/about`** page (soleat.net/about) + apex route (no more `/`-404); **`/cost`** owner-gated spend tracker; **dark-mode borders** across all 5 TMAs + a perimeter frame on tablet/desktop; first load runs the **full** search/card; **URGENT TMA auth guard** on the open-from-Telegram screen. *(#813, #819, #901, #979, #986, #1006)*

### F-K · Eatery card redesign (distance, re-order, collapse) — v0.62.114 arc
The result card was reworked into a **collapse-by-default** card. The **straight-line walk-minutes** ("{n} min walk") was dropped and the **distance moved into the meta row** (`⭐4.3 · $$ · Open · 📍 ~1.1 km away`), folding away the old standalone "📍 … away" row; 🥗 Healthier Choice + 🏢 inside-building were merged onto one row. Then a full **row re-order + collapse**: collapsed shows identity · type · meta(★·$·open·📍dist) · price/♿️/🐾/crowd · **🍲 Try**; on expand (`⌄ details / ⌃ less`) the rest reveals in the new order address → ⭐ Recommend → ✳️ Michelin/Bib → 🥗/🏢 → 💬 review → 🌿 vibe → Maps/Copy/socials (last). The focused/selected card **auto-expands**. *(#1070, #1074; mockup #1073)*

### F-L · Music-app result carousel + floating layout — v0.62.115/.125/.127/.128
Selecting a result turns the list into a **music-app carousel**: the picked card **centred + auto-expanded**, neighbours **peeking** left/right, swipeable; the map **recenters / zooms to 17 / blinks** the pin, and **✕ or a tap on the empty map** returns the vertical list (which stays as a scroll-down fallback). A tap on a result card or pin **zooms in (17) + blinks** and **tap-out restores** the prior browsing zoom (cuisine + hawker). Toward a floating layout: the **header is now floating / always-present** (sticky, frosted) and the **carousel docks at the footer, off the map** so the map stays fully visible. *(#1061, #1075, #1078, #1079; mockups #1073, #1077)*

### F-M · "Local food picks" plate — rename + 3-stage collapse — v0.62.116
The geo "What to try here" plate is renamed **"📍 Local food picks:"** (FR "Sélection locale") and collapses in **three stages** (label → one-line `A • B • C` peek → full dish rows → closed). The peek one-liner is **qualifier-led** (`Laksa (Katong)` → "Katong Laksa", `Bak Kut Teh (Teochew)` → "Teochew Bak Kut Teh"). Display-only — the dish name stays the search query. *(#1062)*

### F-N · "Cuisine:" plate — relabel + 3-stage collapse + exclusion — v0.62.123
The cuisine "what to order" plate is relabelled **"Cuisine: {flag}{X}"** (was the wrong "What to order in {X}", since X is a cuisine, not a place) and gets the same **3-stage collapse**. Category chips that are not cuisines (Michelin / Bib Gourmand / Fusion) are excluded via a `NON_CUISINE_PLATE` set, so a category-only or mixed selection never builds a "Cuisine:" plate. *(#1071)*

### F-O · Venue type from `places.types[]` — v0.62.117
The eatery type line now shows the **most specific cuisine subtype** mined from Google's `places.types[]` (e.g. `gluten_free_restaurant` → "Gluten-free") rather than the generic `primaryType`. When all that resolves is a bare "Restaurant"/"Food", the 🍽️ line is **hidden** entirely. *(#1063)*

### F-P · GPS reverse-geocode → building / street — v0.62.118
A shared **GPS** location now resolves to the **building, then the street** (e.g. "115 Bukit Merah View") instead of the planning area ("Bukit Merah"); planning-area names are the fallback. Applies to `/location` and `/transport`. *(#1064)*

### F-Q · Transit links & chips in the venue pop-up — v0.62.120/.130
The eatery card / pop-up's **bus-stop codes are now hyperlinks** (parity with the station links): tapping a 🚌 code pans + flashes the stop and opens its **live-arrivals** popup, staying in the TMA. The nearest-**station codes ride their official MRT line colour** as white-on-colour chips (NS red · EW green · CC orange · DT blue · TE brown · NE purple · BP/LRT grey · JR teal · CR lime); interchanges show one chip per line. *(#1068, #1081)*

### F-R · SG search no longer drifts foreign — v0.62.121
A Singapore-scoped cuisine search no longer leaks **foreign venues** (selecting "Polish" with a SG location had returned Warsaw restaurants). Coordless far-away Places matches are dropped unless they are genuinely local (empty or Singapore address), and the never-empty floor draws only from finite-distance venues within a **120 km** hard cap. *(#1069)*

### F-S · Skeuomorphic map pop-ups + region pills — v0.62.126/.129
The Google-map **pop-up info card** (tapped open from a pin) is now **skeuomorphic** across all three map TMAs — a raised, beveled card (gradient + hairline border + drop shadow + inset highlight). The region picker pills are **equal width**, and the **selected pill in dark mode** reads as **pressed-in / debossed** (accent gradient + inset shadow), driven by a new `data-theme` exposed from the Telegram colour scheme. *(#1076, #1080)*

### F-T · Map default zoom + custom controls — v0.62.119/.132/.133
Bukit Panjang LRT station pins (BP3 Keat Hong, BP4 Teck Whye, BP5 Phoenix, BP7 Petir, BP9 Bangkit) were **corrected to LTA-authoritative coordinates** (they had plotted in the northern loop instead of the southern arc). The default result-map zoom is **11 → 12** across all map TMAs (fit-bounds still wins when results exist). Google's native zoom/pan pad is replaced by a **custom `+ / ↹ / −` cluster** in every map (`/app/map` fullscreen + cuisine/hawker/transport), where **↹ recenters** the map. *(#1065, #1083, #1084)*

### F-doc · Primer, audit & MVP mockups (no app change) — this arc
Self-contained HTML docs served at `/doc/…`: the **Soleat 2-page primer** (`soleat-about.html`), the **UI consistency audit** (`soleat-ui-audit.html`), and four **MVP mockups** (`soleat-local-food-picks-mvp.html`, `soleat-hawker-miniapp-mvp.html`, `soleat-eatery-card-carousel-mvp.html`, `soleat-cuisine-floating-layout-mvp.html`). Static assets only — no version bump. *(#1057, #1060, #1066, #1067, #1073, #1077)*
