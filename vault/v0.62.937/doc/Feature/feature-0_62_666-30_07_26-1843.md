## F-66.1 — Taiwan's Michelin badges now show real 2025/2026 history, not a hardcoded year

Every Michelin badge for a Taiwan venue (chat card, Cuisine TMA card) now reflects the venue's ACTUAL award
history across the two live editions, using the same `retainedAwardYears()` render contract shipped in
v0.62.665 for all 11 non-SG countries. A three-star retained across both editions shows "⭐⭐⭐ · '26, '25"; a
venue promoted between editions (NOBUO, one-star → two-star) shows only "'26" against its new tier; a fresh
debut (Mizue, straight to two-star; the 9 new one-star venues) shows a single "'26".

## F-66.2 — Taiwan's star and Bib Gourmand lists are current through the 2026 edition

Stars: 61 total (3 three-star retained, 9 two-star [7 retained + NOBUO promoted + Mizue debut], 49 one-star [40
retained + 9 new] — cross-checked exactly against the official 2026 ceremony total). 2 one-star venues (Fleur de
Sel, Paris 1930 de Hideki Takayama) did not retain their star and are recorded as 2025-only historical rows, per
this schema's existing history-retention design (see Register D-34). Bib Gourmand: 146 total (up from 143 in
the prior snapshot — officially 144, see Register O-78 for the pre-existing 1-row gap), 13 new, 10 identified
drops recorded as 2025-only rows.
