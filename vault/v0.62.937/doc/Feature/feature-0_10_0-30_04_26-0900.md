## §0.10.0 NEW CONTENT

### 1. Processing Lock (iPadOS loop fix)

Per Human Lead reported observation. A 60-second per-chat processing flag (`proc:<sha256(chatId)>` in Redis) is set when `runFlow` enters and cleared in a `finally` block. Re-entry while the flag is held responds with *"⏳ Gia is still working on your last request — hold on a moment."* and exits without launching a duplicate pipeline.

Covers: impatient `/eat` re-taps, location share retap during processing, typed-place message during processing, default-button tap during processing.

### 2. Hidden Sanctuary (Consultant Layer)

When `pickValidated` returns zero venues, soleat now invokes `findHiddenSanctuary(lat, lng)` before falling through to `handleNoResults`:

1. Places `searchNearby` for any OPERATIONAL + open venue within 300 m (broader `includedTypes`: restaurant, cafe, bar, bakery, food_court, meal_takeaway, point_of_interest), ranked by distance, top 5.
2. For each candidate (in order): fetch reviews → ask Gemini structured-JSON `{is_sanctuary: bool, reason: string}` whether the reviews suggest a quiet, solo-friendly atmosphere.
3. First `is_sanctuary === true` candidate is delivered with the framing:
   > *"I couldn't find a standard {meal} sanctuary, but I've identified a 'Hidden Sanctuary' at {Name} based on recent reviews mentioning {reason}."*

If none qualify, falls through to the normal empty-state `handleNoResults` message.

### 3. Review-Based Filter (extends fail-fast gate)

`validateWithPlaces` now performs an additional check after the existing 200 m, OPERATIONAL, and `openNow` gates: a Place Details review fetch screened against a regex of negative keywords:

```
loud music
extremely noisy / extremely loud
under construction / under renovation
renovation works
closed for renovation
too crowded / overcrowded / over-crowded
packed beyond
```

Any match in last-30-day reviews drops the candidate (with fallback to the most recent 5 reviews if none are within 30 days). Per the v0.8.1 fail-fast policy, this means the **first** Gemini candidate is the gate for *all* filters — proximity, lifecycle, hours, AND review keywords.
