- **[DECISION TREE FOR INTERPRETING THE RESPONSE]**

  - `steps.nl.intent === 'other'` or `confidence < 0.6` → classifier didn't classify as food (unexpected for this query — would indicate Gemini Flash interpreting it strangely).
