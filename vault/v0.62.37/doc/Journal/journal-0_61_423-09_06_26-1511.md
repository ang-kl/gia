## New

### [HDR] #623 | 15:11 SGT 09-06-26 | v0.61.423 — picker field reflects the previewed city (display-only) | App.jsx + LocationField.jsx | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** When you pick a new city in the OTHER picker, the location field must show **that city** — not blank, not the old address.

- **[ROOT CAUSE]** The picker's field / compact pill read the **committed anchor** (`locationAnchor`). But a city-dropdown pick is a **v0.61.354 PREVIEW** (`selectedCityLocation`) that does **not** commit the anchor — so after picking a city the field showed the **stale committed anchor** (old) or blank, while the country + city dropdowns showed the new pick. Hence the "country + city + location(old)" mismatch.

- **[DELTA]** (`web/cuisine/`, **display-only** — the preview / commit / "Back to last search area" model is untouched)
  - Thread `selectedCityLocation` → `LocationField` → `OtherLocationPicker` as the **`selectedCity`** prop.
  - The picker computes `displayLocName = previewName(selectedCity) || committedName(anchor)` and uses it for **`showCompact`**, the **compact-pill text**, and the **resting label / input placeholder**.
  - Result: selecting a city **immediately shows that city** in the field. **🔍 still commits + searches** the previewed city (`triggerSearch` unchanged). Folded the country-name guard into `displayLocName`; removed the now-dead `anchorNameSafe` + `restingLabel`.
  - **`package.json`** `0.61.422 → 0.61.423`; **`doc/.serial-state.yml`** commit 635 → 636, journal 622 → 623, **build 403 → 404**; re-anchored 15:11 SGT.
