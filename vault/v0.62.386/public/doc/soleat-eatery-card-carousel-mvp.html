2 The music-app carousel (selected card)

Tap a card to select it → the view lifts into a "now playing" layout: map fills the top half centred on the venue's numbered pin; the selected card sits below, expanded; the previous / next eateries peek at the left/right edges and are swipeable. Tap outside (or the map background) collapses back to the fixed vertical list, scrolled to that card.

4 Build shape

  • Card (low risk): reorder the JSX in ResultCard.jsx + add a collapsed/expanded state (collapsed shows rows 1–4; a tap toggles). Pure presentational.
  • Carousel (the big piece): a new selected-card view mode in ResultPanel/App — when a card is selected, render the map (top) + a horizontal snap-scroll track of cards (the same ResultCard, expanded) instead of the vertical list; tap-outside restores the list. Reuses the existing focus/zoom-17/blink wiring.

5 Open questions

  • Collapsed depth: collapse from the address down (as drawn), or keep the 🍲 Try dish visible even when collapsed (it's the most useful "should I tap" cue)?
  • Carousel trigger: tapping the card body selects → carousel; but the card also has Maps/Copy/dish buttons. Confirm a tap on the card background triggers the carousel while buttons keep their own actions.
  • Map height in carousel: ~half the viewport (as drawn) ok, or a third?
  • Swipe vs the map: swiping the card track moves between eateries; the map just follows. Or should the map itself be draggable too?
