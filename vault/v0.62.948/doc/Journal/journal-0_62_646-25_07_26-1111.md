### [HDR] v0.62.646 — the stacking-context bug behind "expand not working", plus the card sizing the operator's own A/B chose

- **[INTENT]** Operator: *"Proceed to build and the expand/collapse Google map layer in train TMA and hawker
  TMA again not working. Station that is future have a tab like cuisine TMA card with the word 'future'. Do

  content-sized rather than uniform.

- **[ROOT CAUSE — map expand, my own regression]** The drawer layouts wrap the map in
  `<div className="absolute inset-0 z-0">`. A positioned element with an **explicit z-index creates a
  stacking context**, so the map panel's fullscreen `fixed inset-0 z-[35]` expand overlay was **trapped inside
  that z-0 box** and could never paint above the **z-30** `BottomSheet` drawer → ⇲ Expand "did nothing" (the
  map *did* go fullscreen — behind the drawer). Introduced in **v0.62.639** (Transport LIST drawer) and
  **v0.62.642** (Hawker portrait drawer); the phone-portrait Transport drawer carried it too.

- **[DELTA]** (3 files)
  1. **Expand fix** — drop `z-0` from all **three** wrappers (`web/transport/src/App.jsx` ×2,
     `web/hawker/src/App.jsx` ×1). `absolute inset-0` is `z-auto`: no stacking context, identical paint order
     in the normal state, and the overlay escapes to the viewport where its `z-[35]` beats the drawer's
     `z-30`.
  2. **Content-sized card** — the fixed collapsed height (`h-[14rem]` in v0.62.645; `h-[27vh]` before that) is
     **removed** from `StationCard`. The operator's comparison showed Cuisine's cards are each sized by their
     content and read better for it, while the Train card was ~⅓ content and ⅔ dead space with the ▾ pill
     stranded at the bottom. Train cards are now content-sized in **both** states, like Cuisine and Hawker.
  3. **"Future" tab** — a station with `status === 'future'` now gets a **Cuisine-style folder tab** above the
     card (same construction as `ResultCard`'s "Closed" tab: rendered **outside** the bordered card so its
     rounded corner can't slice the tab, `-mb-1` tucking it under the top edge, `ml-3` offset), carrying the
     word from `mrt.future`. The old inline "(future)" marker beside the name is retired. CVD-safe — the
     **word** carries the meaning.
