# Journal — v0.62.696 (Michelin ticks: a popup, a nudge, and a year that could never work)

_(№ 1,035 — 01-08 '26 19:55 SGT)_

## New

### [HDR] v0.62.696 — the Michelin year / Bib ticks move to a modal, and start doing something

- **[INTENT]** Operator, in Cuisine → "Cuisine & filters":
  *"(1) Select 'Michelin · Bib Gourmand' it should pop-up same window '2026', '2025', 'Bib Gourmand'
  like how 'Sweets & Fusion' takes effect when selected. Remove current showing of '2026', '2025',
  'Bib Gourmand' on the same screen of 'Cuisine & filters'.
  (2) By the way, none of the '2026', '2025', 'Bib Gourmand' works. check your wiring."*
  Then: *"run test that when select Michelin Star 2026 or 2025 or both plus/- Bib Gourmand, all the
  respected Michelin Star of that countries will appear like it does before … try different
  countries"*, and on the follow-up question:
  *"#2 - follow the existing convention and just nudge you to search?"*

- **[THE WIRING WAS SOUND — checked end to end before changing anything]** "Check your wiring" was
  answered by testing it, not by reading it:
  - **Chips → callback**, in a real browser against the real component:
    ```
    initial    mf=null                                          2026=✓ 2025=✓ Bib=✓
    click 2026 mf={"year2026":false,"year2025":true,"bib":true}  2026=✗ 2025=✓ Bib=✓
    click 2025 mf={"year2026":false,"year2025":false,"bib":true} 2026=✗ 2025=✗ Bib=✓
    click Bib  mf={"year2026":false,"year2025":false,"bib":false}2026=✗ 2025=✗ Bib=✗
    ```
  - **Server filter → pool**, running `michYearMatches` (index.js ~9718) against the real datasets
    for **all 11 countries**:

    | | SG | MY | TH | JP | KR | TW | VN | HK | MO | PH | CN |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | all on | 140 | 69 | 180 | 587 | 117 | 219 | 83 | 147 | 34 | 34 | 475 |
    | 2026 only | **0** | 9 | 43 | 359 | 46 | 61 | 11 | 77 | 21 | 9 | 129 |
    | 2025 only | 41 | 7 | 33 | 70 | 9 | 52 | 9 | 18 | 8 | **0** | 20 |
    | Bib only | 99 | 60 | 137 | 228 | 71 | 156 | 72 | 70 | 13 | 25 | 326 |

    Every combination narrows correctly, and the default (all on) returns the full pool everywhere —
    so the operator's real worry, *"all the respected Michelin Star of that country will appear like
    it does before"*, holds. All 140 SG entries carry `awardYears`; nothing is silently dropped.

- **[SO WHY DID IT LOOK BROKEN? TWO CAUSES, NEITHER IN THE "WIRING"]**
  1. **Changing a tick never triggered anything.** `onMichelinFilterChange` did exactly one thing —
     `setState` — and nothing prompted a re-search. The new filter sat in state until the user
     happened to press 🔍 again. Tick a box, watch the screen, nothing happens.
  2. **In Singapore the 2026 tick could never matter.** Look at the SG column: **0 stars carry
     `'26`**. Singapore's 2026 STAR selection is unannounced (ceremony 04-08 '26) — v0.62.665
     recorded exactly that — so all 41 SG stars are `'25`-only. Toggling 2026 in Singapore is a
     guaranteed no-op *even after* a fresh search. Anyone testing from the default SG region would
     conclude the whole feature was dead, which is precisely what happened.

- **[DELTA — (1) the popup]** New `MichelinFilterDrawer.jsx`, the same modal shape
  `CuisineCategoryDrawer` uses (scrim + centred card + ✕ + `useDialog` focus trap). Selecting the
  Michelin chip opens it; de-selecting closes it. The inline row is **removed** from `CuisineDrawer`
  — it had put a third kind of control on a screen that is otherwise category chips, and it stayed
  on screen for as long as Michelin was selected. Prior markup lives in git at v0.62.695 rather than
  as commented-out code.

- **[DELTA — (2) the nudge]** Per the operator's choice, ticks do **not** auto-search. Changing one
  now raises the **same search hint** a category-drawer close raises, so the convention is identical
  to the cuisine chips: change criteria → prompted to search → search applies them.

- **[DELTA — (3) an honest 2026]** New `michelinYearsByCC` on `/api/cuisine/catalogue`: the award
  years each country actually holds (SG from `SG-michelin.js`'s flat `awardYears`, the other 11 via
  `michelin-data.js` `retainedAwardYears()`). The popup **greys a year with no holders**, strikes it
  through, and captions *"Greyed years have no entries for this country yet."* Fails **open** on a
  null list — the same convention the server already uses for a null `michelinCuisines` allow-list.
  This is the part that actually answers "none of them works" for a Singapore user.

- **[VERIFICATION (sandbox)]** Popup driven in headless Chromium across three data shapes:

  | scenario | inline ticks | popup on select | 2026 | 2025 | Bib |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `availableYears = null` (fail open) | **0** | ✅ | on | on | on |
  | **Singapore** (`['25]`) | **0** | ✅ | **disabled + struck** | on | on |
  | Japan-like (`['26,'25]`) | **0** | ✅ | on | on | on |

  Clicking a chip emits the expected object in every case; no page errors. Cuisine build green;
  `node --check index.js` clean; full `npm test -- --run` **156 files / 3901 tests** green.

- **[STATUS]** DRAFT — branch `claude/handover-july-11-49uzvf`.

- **[KNOWN GAPS]**
  - The popup was driven with a **fixture** catalogue shaped from the server's own `categories.push`
    block. Two earlier fixture attempts were wrong (`children` vs `cuisines`, then an empty
    `cuisines: []` that opened the category drawer instead) — both were caught because the probe
    printed what it found rather than asserting success.
  - `michelinYearsByCC` is computed per catalogue request. It is cheap (a set-union over already-
    loaded arrays) but it is not cached; if the catalogue endpoint ever becomes hot this is a
    candidate to memoise alongside `michelinCuisinesByCC`.
  - The year ticks remain **hardcoded to 2026 and 2025**. A country whose latest guide predates 2025
    would show two greyed years and no way to reach its stars. None of the 11 is in that state today
    (every one holds `'25` or `'26`), but the control does not generalise. Recorded as **O-124**.
  - No device confirmation of the modal's appearance.
