- **[WHY IT'S BEHAVIOUR-IDENTICAL].** `require()` is module-cached, so one shared binding is the same

  `resolveLang` is defined for all 37 former call sites (all below `:43`). Aliased forms keep their own
  separate bindings and are unaffected.
