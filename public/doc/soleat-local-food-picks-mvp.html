"Local food picks" — rework MVP

A global-first redesign of the arrival plate: city picks everywhere, with an area "near here" layer that adds Bib Gourmand (wherever the data exists) and a Singapore-only nearby-hawker-centre choice.

Mockup only — NOT built · proposed for v0.62.x · base = the shipped 3-stage plate (v0.62.116) · data: city-plates.js, SG-michelin.js (Bib), data/hawker-centres.json

1 The principle — three honest layers

① City picks — the curated dishes for the matched city (platesForCity / platesNear). Global, unchanged. Tokyo gets Tokyo dishes, Bangkok gets Bangkok dishes. This is the spine — it fixes "too Singapore-centric."
② 🏅 Bib Gourmand near here — nearby Bib Gourmand entries from the recognised registry, scoped to the anchor. Works in any city the guide covers (SG today; extends as other cities' Bib data lands). The curation you liked, surfaced by proximity.
③ 🍜 Hawker centres near here SG ONLY — the 2–3 nearest hawker centres flagged as a choice (tap → open in the Hawker mini-app / locate). Gated to Singapore mode; hidden everywhere else.

Layers ② and ③ only render when they have content for the anchor; otherwise the plate is just the global city picks — never an empty or SG-shaped shell abroad.

2 The plate, stage by stage

Collapse behaviour is the shipped 3-stage toggle (tap to advance, tap again at full to close). The new layers live inside stage 2 (full).

All three read existing curated, sourced data — consistent with the AU "no invented dishes at runtime" rule. The earlier idea of mining hawker tags[] as dish names is dropped: per your steer, the SG hawker layer is a centre choice, not a dish list, and the Bib entries carry the curated quality.
