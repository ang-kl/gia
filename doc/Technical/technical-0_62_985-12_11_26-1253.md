## 5. Constants that bound cost

Unchanged from № 74 §6 except that a tapped plane not on the board now costs one `ETA_CREDITS` (8) call per callsign
per `ETA_CLIENT_TTL_MS` (5 minutes), and never for a plane on the ground or after an "unavailable" answer.
