## Audit finding (before implementation)

`dish-food-group.js`'s `foodGroupFor()` classifies dish type by regex over **SG/Malaysian hawker
vocabulary only**. Checked every cuisine: **52 of ~70 are over 50% "other"**, many at **100%**
(French, Korean, Spanish, Mexican, Lebanese, Portuguese, Brazilian, Argentinian, and more) — dishes
like "adobo", "paella", "kimchi", "tacos" match none of the rules. Not a Filipino-specific bug; a
structural gap.
