- **[KNOWN GAPS]**

  **The DURIAN_PASTRY 18.0 % number deserves the most attention.** Two competing readings:

  - **Reading A — Places API noise.** Places returned 9259 venues for the widened pastry seed set (`durian puff` / `cake` / `dessert` / `ice cream` / `mochi` / `crepe` / `shake` / `smoothie` / `frappuccino` / `snack` / `kueh` / `tart` / `pancake`). Most of those 9259 are generic bakeries, cafes, and dessert shops that the seed terms only loosely match. Our v0.61.262 filter rejected 82 % of them as not-durian-pastry-specific. Under this reading, **18 % is the keep rate, not the precision of the kept list**; the 1668 kept may still be ~85–95 % true durian-pastry venues. A sample inspection of the kept list:
    - 95 unique kept venues have "durian" in the name (e.g. *Sunlife Durian Puffs & Pastries*, *The Durian Bakery*, *DurianZilla*, *Four Seasons Durians*) — high-confidence true positives.
    - 120 unique kept venues do not have "durian" in the name but passed the recency-review canonical gate (≥ 2 mentions in last 24 m within `bakery`/`cake_shop`/`pastry_shop`/`dessert_shop`/`dessert_restaurant`/`ice_cream_shop`). Mostly real durian-pastry specialists (*Temptations Cakes*, *Emicakes*, *Twobakeboys Balestier*) BUT at least one false positive seen on inspection: *Ritz Apple Strudel - Bugis Junction* (apple strudel shop; reviews mention "durian" incidentally).

  - **Reading B — Strict-name + recency-review combo over-rejects.** Operator's earlier framing was that durian pastry shops should be "in the thousands". If true, our kept count of 1668 is low and the filter is wrongly rejecting many real pastry shops whose name doesn't contain "durian" AND whose recent reviews don't repeatedly mention it (perhaps because the shop sells durian only seasonally).

  Both readings can be true at once. The honest answer: we don't know the *true* precision of the 1668 kept list without manual review (or a Gemini-cross-check, which was already in the v0.61.262 follow-up backlog).

  **Two next-step options, decided by operator:**

  1. **Tighten the recency-review path for DURIAN_PASTRY** — bump from ≥2 to ≥3 mentions, OR require `mentions / reviewCount >= 0.5`. Drops borderline false positives like *Ritz Apple Strudel*. Trade-off: also drops real specialists whose Places review snippet is small (Places caps at ~5 reviews).
  2. **Gemini-cross-check the 1668 kept** — feed name + primaryType + top 3 review snippets to Gemini Flash, ask "is this a durian-pastry specialist or just a generic bakery that happens to mention durian once?". Then label each venue in the JSON. Costs ~$0.005 per venue ≈ $8 for the full kept list, well under the v0.61.260 G4 cap.

  The DURIAN (fruit) side is operating well at 83.4 % and within striking distance of the 90 % realistic target. The remaining ~17 % of rejections are mostly grocery stores and supermarkets whose primaryType wins over the haystack — that's the v0.61.262 known gap, not a regression.

  **Recall: the operator asked for ≥ 95 %.** Neither mode is there yet. Achieving 95 % with pure heuristics may not be feasible; the Gemini-cross-check path is the realistic route.
