### [HDR] #217 | 20:16:00 SGT 17-05-26 | v0.61.14 — transport focused-line panel: station picker + 6 km station-focus map | ~12 kb | 5 files | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Operator on the Transport TMA focused-line panel: move the `35 stations`
  dropdown inside the status block; show interchange stations with all their codes
  (`EW16 · Outram Park ⇋ TE17 ⇋ NE3`), each code tappable; selecting a station shows its
  status below the status line and re-centres the Google map on it, with a 6 km radius of
  all-line stations opaque and everything beyond faded to 60 % transparent.

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`web/transport/src/data/line-paths.js`** — new `lineStationsFull()` — one row per
    station record on the line, all codes (focus-line code first), `future` flag, full
    record fields, ordered by running order.
  - **`web/transport/src/components/LineStatusPanel.jsx`** — the `<details>` dropdown moves
    inside the status block; summary `{N} stations · All normal`. Rows render the
    multi-code interchange format with each code a tappable line-coloured chip; future
    records tagged `(future)`. A selected-station detail block (each line's service status +
    PCD crowd level) renders under the status line; `×` clears it. Fetches
    `/api/transport/crowd`.
  - **`web/transport/src/App.jsx`** — `focusedStation` state; threaded to both panels;
    selecting a station surfaces the Google Map (PNG→gmap one-shot); a focused-line change
    clears the station selection.
  - **`web/transport/src/components/MrtMapPanel.jsx`** — `focusedStation` prop drives a 6 km
    station-focus mode: all lines shown, pins/line within 6 km opaque, beyond 60 %
    transparent, the selected station a larger centre pin; `splitByRadius` + `metresBetween`
    helpers; `fitBounds` to a 6 km box.
  - **`web/transport/src/i18n.js`** — `mrt.allNormal`, `mrt.stationsCount`, `mrt.selected`,
    `mrt.future` (en + fr).
