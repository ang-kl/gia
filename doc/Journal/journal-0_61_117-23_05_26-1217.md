> Operator follow-up to v0.61.112 (#604) and v0.61.116 (#608):
>
> *"the Zoom in the attractions toggled for all TMA including design-TMA
> hungs after zoom in after level 14"* — Cuisine TMA, Hawker TMA, Train
> TMA plus the four bot `/app/map` views (View 3 Train Stations, Compare

> v0.61.112 debounced the **MrtMapPanel**'s `zoom_changed` listener to
> stop the ~200-marker rebuild from stacking on rapid zoom. The
> overlay-controller's `zoom_changed` listener in `mapOverlays.js` —
> which v0.61.116 then loaded the new cluster engine onto — was left
> synchronous. Past z14 the Attractions / Carpark cluster cascade plus
> the amenity (`clinics` / `police` / `hospitals`) `'label'`-tier swap
> ran on every Google-Maps zoom tick of a pinch/wheel gesture, stacking
> until the main thread locked. The bot `/app/map` listener ran on
> `idle` (naturally settled) but its single render still tears down +
> re-creates every visible bus-stop / carpark / attraction / train
> marker, and two `idle` events fired back-to-back when a pinch settled
> + the camera re-anchored — same stacking shape.
>
> This entry is commit 324 / journal 317 / build 120, off `main` at
> v0.61.116 (#608).

## New

### [HDR] #317 | 12:17 SGT 23-05-26 | v0.61.117 — Overlay-controller `zoom_changed` debounce + `/app/map` `idle` debounce | ~6 kb | 5 files | 1 PR | 1 build |

- **[INTENT]** Stop the Attractions / Carpark / amenity render passes
  from stacking per zoom tick. Same shape as v0.61.112's MrtMapPanel
  fix, applied to the surfaces v0.61.116's cluster engine actually
  lives on (the overlay controller + the bot `/app/map` view).

- **[DELTA]**

  - **`web/cuisine/src/v2/lib/mapOverlays.js`** (+ byte-identical copies in
    `web/hawker/src/lib/mapOverlays.js` and `web/transport/src/lib/mapOverlays.js`):

    - The `zoom_changed` listener (was synchronous: 6 `applyVisibility`
      calls including `applyClusterAndDrop` for Attractions + Carpark)
      now schedules its body through a ref-held trailing `setTimeout`,
      150 ms delay. Rapid pinch/wheel zoom ticks coalesce into one
      render after the gesture settles. Pattern mirrors the v0.61.112
      `zoomRenderTimerRef` inside MrtMapPanel.
    - New `zoomTickTimer` closure variable inside
      `createOverlayController`; cleared and reset on each
      `zoom_changed`; the debounced callback bails on `destroyed`.
    - `destroy()` clears `zoomTickTimer` so a gesture-in-flight at
      unmount can't fire `applyVisibility` on already-cleared layers.

  - **`public/app.js`**:

    - The `idle` listener (which calls `refreshClippedLayers` — the
      `runClusterPass` for Attractions + Carpark, the bus-stop tier
      rebuild, the `renderTrainStations` pass with `demoteByOverlap`)
      is now wrapped in a 120 ms trailing debounce so the
      sometimes-double-firing `idle` past z14 produces one render, not
      two.

  - **`package.json`** — `0.61.116 → 0.61.117` (PATCH).

  - **`doc/Journal/journal-0_61_117-23_05_26-1217.md`** + this entry.

  - **`doc/.serial-state.yml`** counters
    `commit 323 → 324 / journal 316 → 317 / build 119 → 120`;
    `last_anchor_time` re-anchored to `23-05 '26 12:17 SGT`.
