# Journal — soleat v0.62.533 (Bug fix: case-insensitive exit-name join restores 18 dropped exits)

> Surfaced by the operator's question — *"are you saying you don't have exit coordinates for all
> stations curated in our table records?"* Auditing that turned up a real bug: **4 established stations
> silently had zero exits** because the builder's exit-name join is case-sensitive.

## New

### [HDR] #889 | 04:28 SGT 15-07-26 | v0.62.533 — case-insensitive exit join | 4 files | PR #(pending) |

- **[INTENT].** Fix the exit-coordinate gap that the operator's question exposed — distinct from the
  CCL6 exits (which need external data).

- **[AUDIT].** `stations.json`: **175/183** stations have curated exit coordinates. **8** don't, in two
  buckets:
  - **Bucket 1 — a fixable bug (this PR):** HarbourFront, MacPherson, one-north, Gardens by the Bay —
    their exits **exist** in `station-exits.json` but weren't joining.
  - **Bucket 2 — genuinely no source (still open):** Keppel / Cantonment / Prince Edward Road (CCL6) +
    Punggol Coast — absent from the LTA exit GeoJSON.

- **[ROOT CAUSE].** `scripts/build-station-info.js:293` joined exits by **exact-match**
  `exitsByStation[name]`, where `name` is the canonical `mrt-coords` name — but `station-exits.json`
  keys some stations with **different capitalisation** (`HarbourFront` vs `Harbourfront`, `MacPherson`
  vs `Macpherson`, `one-north` vs `One-North`, `Gardens by the Bay` vs `Gardens By The Bay`). The
  lookup missed, so **18 real exits (6+5+4+3, all with lat/lng) were silently dropped**.

- **[DELTA].**
  - `scripts/build-station-info.js` — added a lower-cased index `exitsByStationLC` and look up
    `name.toLowerCase()` (+9 lines).
  - `data/stations.json` — regenerated, then **scoped to only the 4 fixed stations**: they gain their
    `exits[]` (label + lat/lng + nearest_bus_stop) + computed `exit_centroid`, and lose the
    "no exits in source" `data_quality_note`.
  - `package.json` — 0.62.532 → **0.62.533**.
  - `doc/Journal/journal-0_62_533-15_07_26-0428.md` — this entry.
  - `doc/.serial-state.yml` — commit 1006→**1007**, build 632→**633**, journal 888→**889**; re-anchor
    15-07 '26 04:28 SGT (prior 888 anchor demoted verbatim, AU-7).

- **[SCOPING — kept the diff to the 4 stations].** A raw `build-station-info.js` regen bumps every
  station's `last_updated_at` (~167 timestamp-only stations) **and** sweeps in **9 TEL stations**
  (Great World, Havelock, TE23–TE29) whose committed data is **stale vs the current
  `sg_train_timings.json`** — a *separate* drift. I collapsed the timestamp-only churn (restored
  committed timestamps) and restored the 9 TEL + 3 CCL6 stations to their committed versions (the CCL6
  ones to preserve Parity B #1543's surgical timings). Net diff = **only the 4 case-fix stations** +
  the builder.
