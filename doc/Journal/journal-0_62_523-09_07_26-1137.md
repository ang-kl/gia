- **[RESEARCH (sourced, agent pass)].**
  - **Telegram publishes no per-country MAU.** Only the ~1 B global total is OFFICIAL. All per-country
    figures are third-party ESTIMATES (worldpopulationreview, demandsage, soax) and disagree by 2–3×
    on several countries. Recorded as an evidence-quality caveat inside the spec.
  - `zh` / `ja` fail a pure reach test: Telegram is **blocked in mainland China** (Great Firewall,
    2015; removed from the China App Store 2024); in Japan **LINE** holds ~80 % of the population.
  - SG-relevance axis (OFFICIAL): mainland China is SG's #1 arrival market (3.08 M, 2024, STB);
    Census 2020 (SingStat) home language — English 48.3 %, Mandarin ~29.9 %, Malay ~9.2 %, Tamil ~2.5 %.
  - **Malay (`ms`)** — a Singapore *official language* — and **Vietnamese (`vi`)** (~Telegram's 7th
    largest base) both outrank `de` on a combined SG-relevance + reach test. `de` is the weakest
    shipped locale on both axes.

- **[DECISION (operator, gate G3)].** Two questions were put, and answered:
  1. *Criterion?* → **Global Telegram reach.** (Not SG relevance; not a hybrid tier.)
  2. *Ship any locale changes?* → **Document only.** No app change.

  The operator therefore **ratifies the existing eight** and elects to **document the tension rather
  than resolve it**. `ms` / `vi` are recorded as deferred candidates, not oversights.
