## New

### [HDR] v0.62.608 — Hawker (phone portrait): the listing becomes a draggable bottom-sheet drawer over a full map

- **[INTENT]** Operator (IMG_3594): on a **phone** in portrait, change the Hawker listing into a
  **Google-Maps-style draggable bottom-sheet drawer** over a full-bleed map — a **subtle centre short
  line** as the drag handle; drag **up** to see more of the list, **down** to collapse / close.
  *"if this is good, then Train TMA can take effect as well"* — Train is a **follow-up**, not in this PR.

- **[DELTA — new component]** `web/hawker/src/components/BottomSheet.jsx`: a self-contained draggable
  sheet (Pointer Events; mouse + touch). Its `top` is driven by a **snap index** — snaps are TOP
  fractions of the viewport height (**0.14 full · 0.48 half · 0.80 collapsed**, initial **half**).
  Dragging the handle snaps to the nearest height on release; **tapping** the handle steps taller (wraps
  at the top back to collapsed). The inner list scrolls **independently** (via `contentRef` /
  `onContentScroll`) — only the handle drags the sheet. Styled with rounded-top, a top border, an
  up-cast shadow, and the **handle** (a `h-1.5 w-10 bg-tg-hint/40` pill).

- **[DELTA — layout]** `web/hawker/src/App.jsx`: new **`drawerLayout()`** — a full-bleed
  `HawkerMapPanel` (`fill`) behind (`z-0`); a **floating header** (title + zone pills) `absolute top
  z-20` (`pointer-events-none` shell with `pointer-events-auto` controls, so the map stays tappable
  around them); the `BottomSheet` holding the centre-card list; and the `FooterDock`. `panelScrollRef` /
  `onPanelScroll` / `panelAtBottom` are reused for the sheet's scroller + the footer ⇡/⇣. The layout
  switch gains **`if (vp.deviceClass === 'mobile') return drawerLayout()`** before `portraitTabletPanel`
  — **tablets keep the two-panel** (more room).
