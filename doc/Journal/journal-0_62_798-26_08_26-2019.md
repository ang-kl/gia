  and then produced 18 correct hits on city-plates. A gate that acquires an exemption list
  every time it is inconvenient is a gate on its way to being switched off.

  Still open: the untranslated-English rule only fires **inside non-Latin scripts**, so
  English stranded in `id`/`de`/`es` remains invisible to it. `bercita rasa nutty` was caught
