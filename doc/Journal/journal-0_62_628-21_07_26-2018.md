## New

### [HDR] v0.62.628 — Train carousel: float compact cards over a full-bleed map (Cuisine parity)

- **[INTENT]** Operator (three desktop screenshots — Train, Hawker, Cuisine on v0.62.627): *"The carousel
  cards in CUISINE TMA and the Google Map aspect size is the standard in DESKTOP TELEGRAM mode (picture)
  and the other 2 TMA (Hawker, Train) follow exactly."*

- **[DIAGNOSIS]** The **Cuisine** standard = a **full-bleed map** with **compact cards floating** over its
  bottom edge (the map shows through the gaps). **Hawker** already matches (full-bleed map + floating
  carousel + `items-end` natural card heights). **Train** was the outlier on two counts:
  1. Its CAROUSEL mode was a **stacked flex-col** (header · map `flex-1` · a **solid card row** below) —
     the opaque strip shrank the map instead of the map filling the viewport.
  2. `StationCarousel` used **`items-stretch`**, so every card grew to the **tallest** card's height,
     leaving a wall of empty white space under the shorter cards (the "cards too tall" look).

- **[DELTA]**
  - `web/transport/src/App.jsx` — (1) `StationCarousel` track `items-stretch` → **`items-end`** (natural
    card heights) + `pointer-events-auto`; (2) the CAROUSEL-mode return restructured from the stacked
    flex-col to the **Cuisine/Hawker pattern**: full-bleed map (`absolute inset-0 z-0`, `mapBlock(true,
    true)` so `navInset` drops the map nav cluster below the floating header), a **floating header**
    (`absolute top-0 z-20`), and a **floating compact carousel** (`absolute inset-x-0 bottom-14 z-30`) over
    the map's bottom edge.
  - `web/transport/src/components/MrtMapPanel.jsx` — the map **expand overlay** `z-30` → **`z-[35]`**. The
    floating carousel + the phone bottom-sheet both sit at `z-30` and paint later in the DOM, so an equal-z
    overlay would hide *behind* them (the same class of bug Codex flagged on Hawker); `z-[35]` sits above
    them and below the `z-40` footer.
  - **Hawker** unchanged — it already conforms.
