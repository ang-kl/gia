# Journal — soleat v0.62.23 (progressive-results Stage 3: first batch auto-grows 6→12 in two streamed waves)

> Approved plan, Stage 3 of 3 (FINAL). Operator design pick (AskUserQuestion, this session):
> **"Server streams 12 in two waves."** On a STREAMING first tap the slash route now fetches a full
> page of 12 (was 6) and reveals it in two waves within the SAME NDJSON response: a `base` event
> with the first 6 cards (the instant "taste"), then an `append` event with the rest — so the list
> auto-grows to 12 with NO second 🔍. Slow fields still arrive as `patch`es for all 12. The 6-card
> first paint is preserved by the wave split, not by a smaller fetch. NON-streaming first taps keep
> the cheaper 6-slice; follow-up taps are 12 either way (unchanged).

> Commit 690 / journal 673 / build 423 (web/ changed). PATCH 0.62.22 → 0.62.23.

- **[INTENT]** Operator: the first tap should land 6 fast then fill to 12 without another tap.
  Design chosen this session: server fetches 12 on a streaming first tap and reveals 6 + 6 in one
  response (vs a client auto-follow-up or a replace-with-next-12).

- **[DELTA]**
  - **`cuisine-stream.js`** — new `appendEvent(venues, extra)` (`{ type:'append', venues }`);
    `assembleFinal` gains an `appendEvs` arg folded in after base, before patches, so
    `base ⊕ appends ⊕ patches` == the full final list.
  - **`index.js`** (slash route) — `wantsStream` lifted to the slice point;
    `sliceCap = (isFirstBatch && !wantsStream) ? 6 : 12` (streaming first tap now takes a full
    page); the thin-pool outer-ring re-fetch `target` follows `sliceCap` (threshold still
    FIRST_TAP_SLICE, so it stays demand-driven — no extra cost on healthy queries). The stream
    flush splits `top` into `base` (first 6) + `append` (rest, omitted when ≤6).
  - **`web/cuisine/src/v2/lib/ndjson.js`** + **`api.js`** — the reader handles `append` (grow the
    list in place, fire `onPatch` so the card list re-renders 6→12); `assembleFinal` mirror folds
    append venues. App.jsx needs NO change — Stage 2's `onBase` paints the 6, `onPatch` (fired on
    append) grows to 12; the awaited `r` is still the full 12-venue payload.
