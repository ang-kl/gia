## Amendments

- **`panel.range` template** — was `Result {start}-{end} · click 🔍
  for next more` (v0.61.170). Now `Results: {known} · Showing
  {start}-{end}`. The `{known}` is a NEW slot fed by App.jsx's
  monotonic `knownTotal` state; `{start}`/`{end}` now refer to the
  CURRENT PAGE within ResultPanel's client-side pagination, not the
  cumulative server-side range.
- **Flash timer** — was 3000 ms (v0.61.79). Now 5000 ms per
  operator: *"flash the 🔍 for five seconds"*. Applies to both the
  result-load flash + the cuisine-category-close flash.
- **Flash visual** — was a `<span>👉</span>` positioned to the
  left of the 🔍 FAB. Replaced with a speech-bubble tooltip
  positioned ABOVE the FAB, with a downward-pointing tail.
  Operator: *"can it truely be a bubble shape text rather than a
  curve rectangle shape text"*.

## Deleted

- **i18n keys**: `panel.first`, `panel.firstTight`,
  `panel.finalRange`, `panel.noMore`, `panel.michelinFirst`,
  `panel.michelinRange`, `panel.michelinFinal` — all v0.61.170
  template variants. Replaced by the unified
  `panel.range`/`panel.all`/`panel.discovering`/`panel.limit`
