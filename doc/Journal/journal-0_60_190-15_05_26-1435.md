- **[INTENT]**
  - **Issue B (in-app card render)** — v0.60.183 wired `priceRangeDisplay` + `allowsDogs` through the response payload AND through the server-side text renderers (`formatVenueBlock` for Copy-All, `formatTechniqueVenueBlock` for /s + /c chat results). Operator's question *"Where are the pet-friendly and cost range in the results template in (a) Cuisine TMA results, (b) the copy-to/copy, (c) free text?"* surfaced the gap. PR 1 closes (a).
  - **Issue C (first-tap latency)** — Cuisine TMA's initial open already fires `/api/cuisine/warm-start` capped at 5 venues. The user's first 🔍 Search tap then fires `/api/cuisine/search` capped at 12 — and Places-side travel-times + BestTime-footfall enrichment fan out per venue, so the first-tap latency scales linearly. Operator: *"Loading of Cuisine TMA is slow … lower the first 6 to load only."* Cap the first 🔍 tap (empty seen-set for chat × criteriaHash) at **6** instead of 12; subsequent taps keep the 12-cap so the per-criteria walk-through still hits the full pool in ~11 pages.
