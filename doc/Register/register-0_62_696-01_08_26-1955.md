## Note

"Check your wiring" turned out to be the wrong place to look, and the only way to establish that was
to test the wiring rather than re-read it: the chips emit correctly, and the server filter narrows
correctly for all 11 countries × 4 combinations. Both halves were verifiably fine.

The feature was still broken from where the operator sat, for two reasons that live on either side of
the wiring rather than in it — **nothing triggered a re-search**, and in the default region **one of
the three controls was physically incapable of doing anything**. Either alone would produce "none of
them works"; together they made it certain.

D-65 is the transferable part. A disabled control that explains itself is honest; an enabled control
that does nothing is a bug report waiting to happen — and this one arrived as exactly that. The
generalisation is uncomfortable: the app currently has no way to know a control is inert, because
availability lives in the data and the UI hardcoded the options. O-124 is that gap, still open.
