## Note

C-109 is the second time in this session that a fix landed on the wrong element and had to be
corrected after the operator re-reported the same symptom (C-96 was the first, on the pager's two
buttons). Both share a shape worth naming: **the analysis was right and the application was aimed at
the wrong node.** In C-96 it was one of two sibling buttons; here it was an inner child instead of
the outer surface. Neither was catchable by build or tests — only by the operator looking at the
device again. That is the same gap O-103 records, now with a second instance.

Also worth recording from C-108: the liquid-glass "absence" was not a regression at all. The design
did exactly what it said — opaque when in focus, glass when peeking — and the wide-screen case simply
falls entirely into the first branch. A rule can be correct and still produce an unintended result at
a viewport nobody checked it against.
