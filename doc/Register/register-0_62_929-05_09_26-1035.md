## Why this snapshot exists — a new decision, not a staleness refresh

The staleness budget is 150 patch versions and the gap to № 223 is **three**. This snapshot is not
due on those grounds and is not being cut on them.

It exists because **a decision was made** — D-210 — and `__tests__/register-current.test.js`
refused the release until it was filed. The census assertion, written after № 222 shipped a
retraction carrying its own fourth error, reported `D-210` as *stated in `doc/Journal/` and absent
from `doc/Register/`* on the ordinary path, against its own author, before any reader saw it. That
is the guard doing exactly the job it was written for.

**A snapshot cut to record a decision is a different thing from one cut to look current.** The
budget answers *is the folder stale*; this answers *does every decision have a home*.

## New this entry — D-210

| # | Decision | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| D-210 | **A merge record never gets a pull request of its own** — not held for one, not eventually shipped as one. It rides substantive work, or it waits in the Journal, unpushed, until substantive work arrives. | Approved 04-09 '26 from three stated options, the strictest chosen: *"Stop holding — ship records with the work only"*. Tightens D-205, which stopped the docs-only PR but not the record from being **owed** — so the loop ran at one PR per cycle instead of two, and #1863 shipped `[AMD-211]` alone and thereby owed `[AMD-212]`, a record of a record. |

⚠ **The accepted cost, recorded rather than left to be discovered.** A record can now sit unpushed
indefinitely, and this repo runs in **ephemeral containers** — `CLAUDE-protocol.md` §1 calls an
unpushed count the one thing that loses work. It was stated in the option before it was chosen.
