## Note

This is the second self-inflicted regression this session, and both share a shape worth naming.

In v0.62.692 the Hawker white-screen came from `isShort` being used in a function that never declared
it. Here, `z-[35]` was carried into Cuisine along with a sentence explaining why it was safe — and the
sentence was about a different app. In both cases the code compiled, the suite passed, CI went green,
and the defect was only visible to someone using the app.

The specific failure is worth separating from the general one. The general one is that **a comment can
launder an unchecked assumption into an apparent fact**: I wrote "stays under the footer dock (z-40)"
in Cuisine's MapPanel, and that read as verification in every subsequent review, including my own. The
z-index was wrong for a mechanical reason that one grep would have caught; the comment is what made
the mistake look considered.

O-130 exists because the underlying condition — two apps with different, undocumented stacks — is
still there, and the next port has nothing to check against.
