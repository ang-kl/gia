## Note

Three observations, and the useful pattern is that **each needed a different answer**: one was a real
bug with a precise cause (the border was gated on a tap), one was real but mis-estimated (15 % asked,
30 % actually available), and one was not a defect at all (the glass was working exactly as specified,
verified per card).

Answering all three the same way — by adjusting values until the screenshot looked right — would have
fixed one, overshot or undershot the second, and broken the third. Measuring first is what separated
them, and it took one harness run.

That harness has now been built and discarded four times in this session (v0.62.692's crash, the
v0.62.693 geometry, the loop-clone mismatch, and this). O-120 has stopped being a proposal and is
simply overdue.
