## 2. Per-Feature Detail

### F-72.1 — Flightradar24 at Changi and Seletar (v0.62.975, #1918)
- **Behaviour:** "✈️ Airports" in the Train map's ⋮ menu works like the Park row: a ▾ list of Changi and Seletar, shown
  only with access. There were no airport pins in this release (they came in v0.62.978, F-72.4). An airport opens a
  glass movements board in the bottom dock in place of the station cards: Departures and Arrivals, Cards or List,
  sorted by time, flight or airline, headed *"actual times, not the schedule"*. "Show planes" draws the planes near
  the airport on request; tapping an in-air row locates the flight and pans to it; a landed row shows details with no
  request. While a board is open, arrivals are checked every 7 minutes for a gate.
- **Owner-only first:** every route answers `403 owner-only` without calling FR24 unless the verified Telegram user is
  the owner or `FR24_PUBLIC` is exactly `'1'` (operator, 05-11 '26: *"owner-only first"*, spec A3). FR24's Explorer
  terms are still unread; see Register № 227, R-18.
- **Cost guards:** per-request caches, at most 8 requests a minute, credits counted per Singapore calendar month and
  refused at `FR24_MONTHLY_CREDIT_BUDGET` (25,000); the owner's `/fr24usage` reports them.
- **Known gap:** FR24 publishes no terminals, schedules or delays. Changi's G and H gates are labelled T4 from a
  hand-sourced table; no other gate is labelled.

### F-72.2 — Airports ▾ entries tappable (v0.62.976, #1919)
- **Behaviour:** in the ⋮ menu, the Airports submenu entries open their airport while the row itself is unticked
  (`pickWhenOff`). The Park submenu is unchanged.

### F-72.3 — Our own plane, flown path and line to destination (v0.62.977, #1919)
- **Behaviour:** a tapped plane is drawn as the app's own yellow plane, rotated to its heading; its flown path is
  dotted (FR24 `flight-tracks`, cached 2 minutes per flight) and a dashed line runs to the destination airport (from
  the OurAirports table, which gained coordinates).
- **Why:** operator, 05-11 '26, on production with a screenshot of a plane located over Sabah with no sign of where it
  had come from.

### F-72.4 — Pins always shown; estimated arrival (v0.62.978, #1920)
- **Behaviour:** a Google pin with ✈ inside and the airport's name below it, at Changi and Seletar, always on the map
  for a reader with FR24 access (owner-only today); a tap opens that airport's board (operator's answer, 05-11 '26:
  *"Always, tap opens board"*). Google's own T2 / T3 base-map icons stay untappable (`clickableIcons: false`). Arrivals carry FR24's estimated arrival instead of `--:--`
  (*"Real ETA, 8 credits each"*). A plane on the ground gets a single-✈ card and no
  route line.

### F-72.5 — A station not open yet starts no trains (v0.62.979, #1921)
- **Behaviour:** tapping a station whose status is `future` (Bedok South, Sungei Bedok, Xilin until 26 December 2026)
  shows its card and starts nothing: it is not added to the run drawer and its lines' simulated trains do not start.
  The same guard runs in Hawker and Cuisine (`station-sim-controller.js`).
- **Closes:** Register № 226, R-17.

### F-72.6 — One planes request at a time; ETA by callsign (v0.62.980, #1922)
- **Behaviour:** "Show planes" refuses a second request while one is in flight, and "busy" keeps the planes already
  drawn. The ETA call asks for the board's own callsigns (sorted, deduplicated, at most 15) rather than every flight
  bound for the airport, and makes no call without one. An in-air row with no ETA reads `🛫 ≈HH:MM` in place of
  "seen HH:MM".

### F-72.7 — Changi pin at Terminal 1 / Jewel (v0.62.981, #1922)
- **Behaviour:** the Changi pin moved from OurAirports' reference point (1.35019, 103.994), between the runways and
  1,002 m from Changi Airport MRT, to LTA bus stop 95029 "Changi Airport Ter 1" beside Jewel (1.36106, 103.99081). Seletar keeps its reference point; its passenger terminal has no sourced coordinate.
- **Why:** operator, 07-11 '26, on Telegram Desktop at zoom 18 over T2 / T3: the pin was nowhere near a terminal.

### F-72.8 — MICHELIN Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang 2027 (v0.62.982, #1923)
- **Behaviour:** the Malaysia table carries the 2027 guide (announced 04-11 '26): 1 two-star, 9 one-star, 60 Bib
  Gourmand, 70 awards; Kuala Lumpur 1 / 7 / 26, George Town 0 / 2 / 34.
  - 66 continuing venues gain a 2027 award at the same tier; Serabai Istimewa returns to Bib (2025, 2027).
  - Four new rows: Potager (one star), 7 Chai Ipoh Hor Fun, Minji Bak Kut Teh, Lemon Koay Teow Soup (Bib).
  - Heun Kee (closed) and Ah Boy Koay Teow Th'ng keep 2025 and 2026 as history.
  - The 2027 tick in the Cuisine Michelin drawer comes from the data: live for Malaysia, greyed elsewhere.
- **"Dropped" per country:** `latestEditionYear(cc)` judges a venue against its own country's newest edition. The old
  global maximum year would have marked every other country's 2026 venues as dropped.
- **Caveats the operator accepted (spec A2, A3):** the four new rows' addresses come from secondary sources
  (guide.michelin.com returns an empty body from the cloud), and their halal and vegetarian flags are inferred.
- **Not verified from a container:** the 2027 tick on a phone (the catalogue route needs Telegram login).
