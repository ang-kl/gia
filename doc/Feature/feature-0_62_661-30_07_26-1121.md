## F-63.1 — iPhone landscape + list mode: map on top, list scrolls below, no drawer

In every map TMA (Cuisine, Hawker, Transport), switching to list mode while an iPhone is in landscape
orientation no longer opens the shared over-the-map `BottomSheet` drawer — it shows the map anchored in a
fixed top box and the station/venue/hawker-centre list in its own independently-scrolling panel below,
scrollable both up and down. This is scoped to exactly that one device+orientation+mode combination: portrait
phone, and any tablet/desktop orientation, still get the drawer exactly as before. The rationale is
geometric — a landscape phone's ~375–430px viewport height means even the tightest drawer peek covers a
disproportionate share of the map, which the static split avoids by giving each region a fixed, honest share
of the screen instead of layering one over the other.
