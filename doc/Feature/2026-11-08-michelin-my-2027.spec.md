# Michelin Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang 2027 in the Malaysia table

## Intent
- Outcome: the Malaysia Michelin table carries the 2027 edition (announced 04-11 '26): 1 two-star, 9 one-star,
  60 Bib Gourmand, 70 awards. A 2027 tick appears in the Cuisine Michelin drawer for Malaysia, and the venues
  that left the guide stop counting as current. No other country changes.
- Force: MUST. Operator, 08-11 '26, verbatim: *"add Michelin MY 2027"*, after *"confirm - 2027 Michelin list for
  Malaysia"* (№ 4,894 reported the list).
- Done when: the verify command passes; `venuesForYear(2027)` has 70 MY venues with the tier counts above; the
  country and city manifests both assert 2027; `awardsDiff` on a Thai, Taiwanese or Japanese venue current in 2026
  still reports no `droppedAfter`; the mutations listed below are caught.

## Interpretation
- UNDERSTANDING:
  - The table changes are (sources: Malay Mail and The Star, 04-11 '26; SAYS full list; Michelin press release
    via Macau Business):
    - **New one-star:** Potager (KL), on the Selected list before, so new to this table.
    - **New Bib Gourmand, new rows:** 7 Chai Ipoh Hor Fun (KL), Minji Bak Kut Teh (KL), Lemon Koay Teow Soup
      (Penang).
    - **Back to Bib Gourmand:** Serabai Istimewa (Penang) is already in the table, with a 2025 Bib only. It gets
      a 2027 award. No new row.
    - **Not in 2027:** Heun Kee Claypot Chicken Rice (Pudu), already `status:'closed'`, and Ah Boy Koay Teow Th'ng
      (Penang). Both keep their 2025 and 2026 awards. Neither gets a 2027 award.
    - **Everything else held in 2026 continues in 2027 in the same tier**, Dewakan's two stars included.
  - The counts reconcile:
    - Bib: 58 − 2 (Heun Kee, Ah Boy) + 4 = 60. That is 26 in KL (25 − 1 + 2) and 34 in Penang (33 − 1 + 2), which
      matches the press release's split.
    - One-star: 8 + Potager = 9.
    - Two-star: 1.
    - Total: 70. The table grows from 70 rows to 74 and from 130 awards to 200.
  - `awardsDiff` decides "dropped" against `MAX_EDITION_YEAR = Math.max(...VALID_YEARS)` (michelin-data.js:725),
    which is one global year. If 2027 were simply added to `VALID_YEARS`, every Thai, Japanese, Taiwanese and
    other venue current in 2026 would start reporting `droppedAfter: 2026`, because no other guide has a 2027
    edition yet. So the latest edition becomes **per country**: the newest award year among that country's
    loaded venues. MY becomes 2027 and every other country stays 2026. `awardsDiff(venue, latestEdition?)` takes
    an optional override so a synthetic fixture can name its edition.
  - Year ticks are already data-driven (`michelinYearsByCC` → `retainedAwardYears`, client
    `michelin-years.js`, Register O-124). A 2027 tick appears without client code, live for MY and greyed for
    other countries (D-65). `cuisine-michelin-years.test.js` and `michelin-year-filter.test.js` already carry
    `year2027` fixtures, and they are in the verify run to prove it.
  - The `validateVenue` error message "must be 2025 or 2026" is generated from `VALID_YEARS` so it cannot go
    stale again.
- GAPS: the official guide.michelin.com pages return an empty body here, so the new rows' addresses come from
  secondary sources (see A2). Their halal and vegetarian flags are required booleans in the schema, and they are
  my inference (A3).
