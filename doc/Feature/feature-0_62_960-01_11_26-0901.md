## 2. Per-Feature Detail

### F-70.1 — Halal only when certified (v0.62.949, #1892)
- **Behaviour:** see № 69, Amendment 1. A curated row is halal only when `is_halal_certified` is `TRUE`;
  the halal filter offers certified rows only. Google results never had a halal default.
- **Known gap:** the certified label rests on the curated CSV; the app does not check with MUIS or JAKIM.

### F-70.2 — MRT station exits and LTA bus stops refreshed (v0.62.950, #1893)
- **Behaviour:** both datasets replaced, unedited, with the 29-10 '26 downloads. Hume's two exits now read
  "Hume · Exit 1/2", and nine stations' bare exit labels read "Exit …". Bus stops go from 5,148 to 5,205
  codes. 23 stops that had been named as a station's nearest no longer existed upstream and were replaced.
- **Known gap:** Hume's exits are still tagged to Hillview on the map, because Hume has no coordinate in
  `data/mrt-coords.json` (Technical H-3).

### F-70.3 — NParks parks and SPF police refreshed (v0.62.951, #1894)
- **Behaviour:** parks go from 461 to 462 (Westwood Family Park added), and the simplified overlay from
  276 to 279 shapes. Police stays at 128 points. Five statuses changed, and SPF writes the status into
  the name, e.g. "Bukit Timah Neighbourhood Police Centre (Temporarily Closed)".
- **Known gap:** status lives only in the name, so nothing filters closed posts.

### F-70.4 — "Outside now" (v0.62.952, #1895; v0.62.953, #1896; v0.62.954, #1897)
- **Surface:** the weather chip in Cuisine, Train and Hawker gains a dot. Tapping the number opens the
  sheet, and a strip under the header shows the single most urgent warning, which can be dismissed.
- **Rows:** heat stress (WBGT), lightning within 5 km, PUB flood alerts, air quality (PSI and PM2.5) and
  UV, each naming NEA or PUB with its reading time. Cuisine adds the two nearest carparks within 300 m
  with free lots. The parking row names no agency, as the operator asked.
- **Amendment 1 (v0.62.953):**
  - each row leads with the finding ("No flood near you");
  - "No UV after sunset" and "Indoors" readings;
  - full carparks are hidden;
  - the sheet is capped at 480 px on a wide window;
  - the unit flips °C/°F and is remembered on the device.
- **v0.62.954:** after sunset the UV pill reads "—" instead of "Low".
- **Singapore only:** the sheet, strip and dot appear only inside Singapore.
- **Known gap:** on a normal day the dot is amber, because PSI 51-100 is amber by the approved thresholds.

### F-70.5 — Cuisine result cards take their natural height (v0.62.955, #1898)
- **Behaviour:** the standard tier no longer measures every card and stretches them all to the tallest
  one, so plain cards no longer take a Bib Gourmand card's height. Cards still share a bottom edge and
  are capped at 60 % of the screen. The phone-landscape tier keeps its fixed 7.5 rem.
- **Decision:** supersedes D-51 for the Cuisine standard tier only. Hawker and Train keep uniform heights.

### F-70.6 — Park Connector layer (v0.62.956, #1899; v0.62.957, #1900)
- **Surface:** 🌳 Park on the Cuisine, Train and Hawker maps draws parks and the park connectors in a dotted
  magenta. "Park ▾" lists the named loops, nearest first, and tapping a name locks that loop, showing a 🔒 chip
  on the map. The lock resets when the Mini App closes.
- **Data:** NParks' Park Connector Loop (6 loops) and URA's Master Plan 2025 park connector lines (Round
  Island Route, Rail Corridor and 1,289 other segments).
- **v0.62.957:** loops show at every zoom, thin below zoom 13. Before this, the apps opened at zoom 11-12
  and turning Park on drew nothing, which the operator reported.
- **Parks:** drawn for the visible map rather than a fixed set.

### F-70.7 — Map popups in the reader's language (v0.62.958, #1900)
- **Behaviour:**
  - police and hospital second lines are composed from SPF's and MOH's name patterns, and only when every
    part has a translation;
  - Google's weekday hours are converted;
  - police types and statuses, hospital categories, phone purposes and the four taxi names come from
    tables in 9 locales;
  - the card words (Website, Wheelchair, Exit, Operating and others) are translated.
- **Covers:** police, clinics, hospitals, attractions, taxis, carparks, the bus card and the hawker overlay.
  Word order and spacing follow the script of the joined words.

### F-70.8 — Settings survive a language switch and a reopen (v0.62.959, #1900)
- **Language switch:** keeps the eatery list, the selected eatery, the typed text, the map layers, the Park
  Connector lock and the camera, through a one-navigation stash.
- **Reopen:** keeps settings (filters, map layers, list or carousel, the Train line, the Hawker region) on
  the device. Results still start fresh on reopen, as v0.59.43 decided.
- **Fix:** a Singapore phone with a Tokyo pin no longer restores to an empty list.

### F-70.9 — Weather abroad and NEA's 4-day forecast (v0.62.960, #1902)
- **Chip:** in Train and Hawker the chip uses the reader's own location, as Cuisine's already did.
- **Abroad:** tapping the chip opens a small card with:
  - the condition (translated when it matches a known class);
  - the temperature and humidity;
  - "Heat, lightning, flood, air and UV readings are available in Singapore only";
  - the credit "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com", linked. The Open-Meteo credit had been missing everywhere.
- **Licence:** the Outside-now sheet ends with the Singapore Open Data Licence notice for its NEA and PUB data.
- **Forecast:** "Next 4 days" at the bottom of the sheet, starting tomorrow as NEA issues it. Each row has
  the weekday (the browser's own names), icon, low-high temperature and condition, plus "NEA · issued
  HH:MM". It follows °C/°F.
  - Conditions are labelled from NEA's text, because NEA publishes no code list. Unknown text shows as NEA
    wrote it.
  - Verified live on 31-10 '26 at 23:02: four days from 01-11, issued 17:11.
- **Known gap:** the credit meets Open-Meteo's attribution. Whether the free tier's non-commercial term
  fits Soleat is the operator's open question.
