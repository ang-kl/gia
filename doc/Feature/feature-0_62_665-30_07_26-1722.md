## F-65.1 — Michelin badges now show which years an award was actually held

Every Michelin Star / Bib Gourmand badge (chat card, Cuisine TMA card, Clipboard "clip" card) now shows a
compact, newest-first list of the specific edition years the venue held its CURRENT category — "⭐⭐ · '26,
'25" for a two-star retained across both editions, "✳️ Bib Gourmand · '26" for a stall new to the guide this
year. A venue that changed tier between editions (promoted, demoted, or picked up Bib Gourmand after losing a
star) shows only the year(s) matching its present category — never a year it held something different in,
and never any promotion/downgrade/removal commentary. This applies uniformly across all 12 covered countries
(Singapore + the 11 already-curated others), not just the newly-updated Singapore Bib Gourmand list.

## F-65.2 — Singapore's Bib Gourmand list is current through the 2026 edition

97 establishments (up from 89), reflecting the 28 Jul 2026 official announcement: 10 new stalls, 2 dropped.
Singapore's Michelin Stars remain on the 2025 edition — the 2026 star selection hasn't been announced yet
(ceremony 04 Aug 2026) — and will not gain a `'26` label until that happens and is verified.
