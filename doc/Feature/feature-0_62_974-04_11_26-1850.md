## 2. Per-Feature Detail

### F-71.1 — Simulated trains (v0.62.961, #1903)
- **Behaviour:** a "🚆 Simulated" toggle in the Train map's layer row. Seven lines draw moving trains; CGL, SLRT
  and PLRT have no timetable data and draw none.
  - The timetable (`train-sim.json`) is built from the operators' published first and last trains alone. Each
    segment's run time comes from the first-train step, else the line's median, else 2 minutes.
  - Tapping a train shows where it is going and its next station, with "simulated" in the card.
- **Honesty:** the trains are never presented as live. A caption says so whenever the toggle is on (F-71.6).
- **Known gap:** no open feed gives real train positions; the simulation is a timetable model.

### F-71.2 — Weather for every city (v0.62.962, #1904)
- **The bug that started it:** Cuisine set to Kyoto showed Singapore's 30.6°C. Picking a city preview sent no
  weather request at all. Fixed: the selected city comes first.
- **Source:** MET Norway's Locationforecast replaces Open-Meteo, so no non-commercial source remains. It is cached
  per 0.1° cell until MET's own expiry, never under 30 minutes.
- **Time zones:** every one of the 284 cities Cuisine offers gets its own zone (Australia by state, Indonesia's
  WITA cities, China's single zone). Before, 123 of them fell back to a longitude band.
- **Abroad:** the "Outside now" sheet opens abroad too, with the local reading and forecast.
- **Readable ranges below zero:** "-9 / 1°C" instead of "-9-1°C".

### F-71.3 — JMA warnings (v0.62.962, #1904; v0.62.963, #1905)
- **Behaviour:** in Japan, "Outside now" shows the warnings JMA has in force for the reader's area, located on
  JMA's own warning map.
- **v0.62.963:**
  - quiet offices are seeded from JMA's 7-day feed, at most once an hour;
  - "Snow" and "Sleet" are labelled;
  - a bulletin over 24 hours old reads as "no recent";
  - times show Japan time, with the date when not today.

### F-71.4 — Keyboard toggle and remembered entries (v0.62.964, #1906)
- **Behaviour:** 💬 opens the free-text box with the keyboard up. ⌨️ hides and shows the keyboard while the box
  stays open.
- **History:** the last 30 entries, newest first, repeats moved up. Kept in Telegram's CloudStorage where the
  client supports it, otherwise on the device. Each can be removed, or all cleared.
- **Known gap:** behaviour depends on the Telegram client's web view; the fallback blurs if the keyboard does not
  hide.

### F-71.5 — A saved place locks for 6 hours (v0.62.965, #1907)
- **The report:** `/cuisine` kept opening on yesterday's Kyoto from Singapore.
- **Rule (one, shared by Cuisine, Menu and the chat `/cuisine`):**
  - a place set on purpose stays for 6 hours;
  - after that, if the phone is more than 50 km away, the phone's location wins;
  - a "↩ Back to <place>" chip returns to the saved place.
- **Chat:** the reply says when the place was set ("set yesterday 4:10 PM"), or that the phone will be used.

### F-71.6 — Simulation caption in the header band (v0.62.966, #1908)
- **Behaviour:** the caption moved from a pill over the map into the header, beside the weather strip, in a
  neutral blue-grey. A ✕ hides it for the session; switching the toggle off and on brings it back. When nothing is
  drawn it also says why (paused, out of hours, no data).

### F-71.7 — A bus route and the buses on it (v0.62.967, #1909; v0.62.968, #1910)
- **Behaviour:** in a bus stop's bubble, tapping a service draws its route along the roads and the buses on it now.
- **Shape:** Google Directions draws the road, cached. Where Directions cannot draw a part, that part is drawn
  straight (v0.62.968) instead of losing the whole route; a failure is logged and not paid for twice within an hour.
- **Buses:** one LTA reading per route every 20 seconds, shared by everyone viewing it.

### F-71.8 — Tap a station or a stop (v0.62.969, #1911; v0.62.970, #1912)
- **Station:** tapping a station starts simulated trains on its lines.
- **Stop:** tapping a stop shows every service's 2 coming and 2 going buses, refreshed every 30 seconds. LTA calls
  are capped at 12 at once.
- **Apps:** first the Train app, then Hawker and Cuisine through shared code, so all three behave alike.

### F-71.9 — The run drawer and service hours (v0.62.971, #1913)
- **The report:** the footer chips covered essential information.
- **Buttons:** 🚇 n and 🚍 n sit in each app's footer row, just before back, while something is listed. The footer
  keeps its height.
- **Drawer:** it slides up over the map at 70% width (15% each side), up to 45% of the screen tall, and scrolls.
  - Up to 5 stations and 5 stops; a 6th drops the oldest; a ❌ on each row.
  - Tapping another stop adds it. The newest stop shows coming and going buses; older stops show coming buses only.
- **Service hours:** outside them nothing moves. The header says, for example, "NSL Normal train operations will
  resume at 05:30" and "SBS Transit operations will resume at 05:30", with the date when not today. Weekend times
  apply on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. A disruption shows LTA's own text, never an invented time.
- **Fixed in passing (v0.62.970 defect):** Hawker drew its simulation caption twice.

### F-71.10 — "Experimental" without "Singapore" (v0.62.971, #1913)
- **Behaviour:** every Mini App's footer tag reads "Experimental · v…" in nine languages. Cuisine's region word
  (Singapore, Johor or Others) left its tag too.

### F-71.11 — Map buttons below the header (v0.62.972, #1914)
- **The report:** "cannot see the map buttons in train.TMA", with a screenshot.
- **Measured:** in the List layout the layer row sat at 9 px under a header ending at 134 px; with the resume
  strip (193 px) Reset and Zoom in were covered too.
- **Behaviour:** the app measures the header and places the row 6 px below it and the zoom buttons 36 px below the
  row. On the expanded map both return to the top. Other layouts and the other apps are unchanged.

### F-71.12 — No "Traditional / Non-AI results" line (v0.62.973, #1915)
- **The question:** *"Is this line necessary? … how does it help user"*. It flagged the default mode on almost
  every search, with nothing to act on. The operator chose to remove it.
- **Behaviour:** gone, with the state, the reload field and the nine strings that only fed it. The Quick / AI
  lever still shows the mode.

### F-71.13 — TEL5 / DTL3e open on 26 December 2026 (v0.62.974, #1916)
- **Source:** LTA, 04 Nov 2026: *"will open for passenger service on 26 December 2026"*, for TE30 Bedok South,
  TE31/DT37 Sungei Bedok and DT36 Xilin.
- **Before the date:** the map popup and the location field say "Opens 26 December 2026" (French "26 décembre
  2026").
- **On the date:** from 00:00 SGT every surface that lists operational stations includes them, with no deploy:
  the server (worked out per request), the line drawings (cached per Singapore day) and the built-in lists.
- **Known gaps:**
  - LTA gives no start time, so they open from midnight.
  - A page left open across midnight switches on its next load.
  - Simulated trains on the new stretch wait for the operators' first and last train times, as Circle Line
    Stage 6's did.
