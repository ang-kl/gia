## 2. Per-Feature Detail

### F-69.1 — A foreign location keeps its country (v0.62.938, #1875)
- **Behaviour:** setting a location with `/location <place>` or from the recent-locations list now stores
  the place's country with it, so later searches are scoped to the right country. Fixes two of the three
  paths named in a Codex P1 on #1870; the third is F-69.2.

### F-69.2 — A shared GPS pin abroad gets a country (v0.62.939, #1877)
- **Behaviour:** a pin outside Singapore's 120 km gate takes the country of the nearest curated city
  (`city-centroids.js`) when it lies inside that city's radius, marked `countryInferredFromCoords`.
  Singapore and JB pins are untouched. **Cost:** none — offline lookup, chosen over a paid reverse geocode.

### F-69.3 — The Mini Apps follow a Telegram theme switch (v0.62.940, #1878)
- **Behaviour:** each of the five Mini Apps listens for `themeChanged` and re-applies only its palette, so
  `ready()`, `expand()` and full-screen are not re-run on every flip.

### F-69.4 — Quick / AI lever (v0.62.941, v0.62.942, v0.62.943; #1879, #1880, #1885)
- **Surface:** a small lever inside the Tell-me bar, between 💬 and the text box (moved there in v0.62.943
  after the operator could not find it on the border; 12x18 px since v0.62.942).
- **Behaviour:** Quick sends text to the ordinary free-text search. AI sends it to the natural-language
  route, which reads place, "new" and cuisines from the question and searches 1.5 km around a named place.
  Chat questions in the bot also go to the AI reading. Opening 💬 first, or 10 minutes after the last
  results, folds the cards and blinks "show results".

### F-69.5 — Non-AI results are flagged (v0.62.943, #1885)
- **Outputs:** the results footer reads "Traditional / Non-AI results" in nine locales when the results
  came from the ordinary search; AI results carry no flag; restored results keep their origin.

### F-69.6 — Curated external eateries (v0.62.944, #1886)
- **Surface:** `/external_update` (owner chat only) opens a 15-minute window; CSV files sent to the bot
  (`Alerts_*`, `DailyBrief_*`) are validated and merged by id into Redis's `ext:` keyspace with every
  column kept. Nothing runs monthly.
- **Behaviour:** the "new" filter, the Mini App's AI route and the chat AI path add curated venues near the
  pin that are NOW OPEN (or a pop-up within its dates), under 3 calendar months from their curated date,
  and meet the other filters. They are listed first with a "🆕 Curated" line.

### F-69.7 — Halal labels for JB and Batam (v0.62.944, #1886)
- **Behaviour:** operator rule: *"treat all JB/Malaysia eateries as halal by default unless it has pork or
  some food against its religion"*; Batam the same. Certified places read "Halal ✓ certified", default-rule
  places read "Halal", and the halal filter includes both.

### F-69.8 — Search inside a named building or road (v0.62.945, #1886)
- **Behaviour:** when the named place is a building or road (from Google's place type, same price tier),
  results list places whose address names it first, then places within 150 m tagged "↗ beyond {name}".
  If both are empty the reply says nothing was found there and shows no cards. Applies to the AI route and
  the normal search. Areas and MRT stations keep the 1.5 km search.

### F-69.9 — AI place fallback and logging (v0.62.945, #1886)
- **Behaviour:** if the AI reading names no place, the normal search's place detector reads it from the
  question. Every AI question reaches `/ftlog`, with one D769 log line recording source, place, place kind,
  "new", cuisines and radius — with no chat id.

### F-69.10 — A dish's home cuisine (v0.62.945, #1886)
- **Behaviour:** "Varenyky dumplings" with no cuisine chosen searches Ukrainian; a `dumpling_restaurant`
  with no dish evidence no longer passes for a non-Chinese dumpling.

### F-69.11 — The Criteria card is no longer covered (v0.62.945, #1886)
- **Behaviour:** the insight strip is hidden while the Criteria card is open (operator's option (a)).

### F-69.12 — Review translation capped (v0.62.946, #1888)
- **Behaviour:** one translation may take 5 s across the model chain and the whole step 6 s per search;
  past either, the original review shows. A late translation is still cached for the next search and is
  never written onto a card already sent.
- **Failure mode it fixes:** a hung Gemini call held the cards for 62,028 ms on 26-10 '26.

### F-69.13 — Public holidays from MOM's consolidated dataset (v0.62.947, #1889)
- **Behaviour:** `holidays.js` reads `d_8ef23381f9417e4d4254ee8b4dcdb176` (2020-2027) under a new cache key.
  The previous dataset id had left data.gov.sg, so every boot had been using the built-in 2026 list, which
  ends on 25-12 '26. Live boot log, 28-10: `[Holidays] 104 SG PH cached (data.gov.sg)`.

### F-69.14 — Hawker closures current (v0.62.948, #1890)
- **Behaviour:** `data/hawker-cleaning-closures-2026.csv` replaced, unedited, with NEA's 28-10 '26 download;
  11 of 123 centres changed. Kim Keat Palm Market is under renovation 14-09 to 13-12 '26 (it had been shown
  open), Telok Blangah Rise from 01-11 to 31-12, and three windows NEA now marks "TBC" are dropped.
